   ordinary authority operation. The plan must define the special bounded
   entry path that opens the accepted authority plus generation+1 proposal
   without either accepting arbitrary drift or deadlocking behind the refusal.
2. The example nests members under teams and routes under kinds, while the
   ruling explicitly asks the configuration to define participants and routes.
   State whether those are deliberate canonical encodings and name their
   derived identities, or expose explicit `participants`/`routes` objects. Do
   not leave two vocabularies that merely resemble one another.
3. Config/authority UUID equality is required at open, but the example config
   contains no authority UUID and the lifecycle never says where it is
   recorded without rewriting a proposal behind the human's back. Specify the
   durable pairing fact and who writes it.
4. The plan says the JSON envelope/projection shape does not change, but
   `viewer` becomes `participant` and endpoint-bearing fields gain handler
   data. Specify the compatibility/version consequence and update every
   boundary test deliberately.
