# WS-2 options response review

Reviewer: `baton.reviewer`
Date: 2026-08-14T21:32:17Z
Disposition: substantially converged; Slawomir ruling still required

K's response to the changes-requested review converges on derived-only due
actionability with an always-visible count, verification as a specialized
exact `@` obligation, refusal of new required edges to closed Work, and a
precise closed-record rule that preserves reads, traversal, historical
references, and personal `mark_seen` hygiene.

One proposed detail is rejected as contradicting the confirmed policy:
verification-flavored obligations cannot be selected as a WS-1
obligation-backed waiting condition. A report must not automatically move
provider phase from `waiting` to `queued`; feedback informs the reviewer, who
must explicitly extend, resume, abandon, or close. The specialized obligation
therefore shares exact endpoint, authorization, identity, and actionable
projection with `@`, but is deliberately ineligible as an automatic Work wake
condition.

One product choice remains. K recommends requiring a terminal outcome only for
Work that has an incoming dependency edge or has ever held a verification
round, and refusing the argument otherwise. The reviewer recommends requiring
exactly `satisfying` or `non-satisfying` on every terminal close. Universal
outcome avoids graph-shape-dependent close syntax and gives parents,
follow-ups, top-level Work, rejected research, and later readers an explicit
statement of whether the terminal conclusion met the Work's purpose. Either
rule is implementable; it must not be chosen mechanically.

Implementation group 1 remains held pending Slawomir's ruling.
