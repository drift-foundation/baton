## R1 — terminal close leaves classic `@` obligations actionable

`close_work` closes Work without inspecting its pending classic obligations.
`respond_obligation` and `dispose_obligation` inspect only obligation state,
not the carrying Work's terminal status. The committed result is closed,
immutable Work with a pending actionable obligation whose route handler may
later append a response message to that closed history.

This contradicts the confirmed rule that closed Work refuses new carrying
discussion or obligation activity and violates the invariant that closure must
not leave actionable loose ends. Additive regression:

`tests/work/test_ws2_close.py::test_terminal_close_never_leaves_a_classic_obligation_actionable`

It currently fails with the obligation still `pending` after close.

**Recommended ruling:** terminal close atomically withdraws every still-pending
exact `@` obligation carried by the Work, removes it from actionable
projections, records the route-resolution/audit facts, and exposes withdrawal
to the owed route. A later report/respond/dispose refuses. This is preferable
to refusing close until recipients answer requests the Current owner no longer
needs. It also gives classic and verification-flavored obligations one honest
terminal cancellation vocabulary without making feedback automatic.

The correction must revalidate pending status and route resolution inside the
close transaction. Race `respond|dispose|report` versus close so exactly one
legal serialization commits; no obligation may be both answered and withdrawn,
and close outcome/dependency wake/withdrawals/audit must remain atomic.

## R2 — claimed follow-up race test never executes its interleaving

`test_a_target_closing_mid_flight_is_valid_at_commit` installs an `_write`
interleaving but calls `create_work(... follow_up_of=open_target)`. The
optimistic precheck refuses before `_write`, so the competing close never runs.
The test name/docstring claims validity-at-commit coverage while its assertion
only proves the ordinary early refusal.

Rename/rewrite it as the early-refusal case, or replace it with an actually
reachable race. Under immutable closure a predecessor observed closed can
never become open, so the in-lock recheck is defense in depth rather than a
live state race; the test and break-sweep report must say that honestly.
