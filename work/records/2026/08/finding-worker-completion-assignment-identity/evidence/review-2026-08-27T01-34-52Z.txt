2. Remaining authorization-to-mount split

Relevant production flow:

  attempts.py:736  request_runtime_start(..., inputs=None)
  attempts.py:783  authorize_input_root(..., inputs=inputs)
  attempts.py:800  adapter.start({labels, operation_id})

The authenticated input path is absent from the adapter request.

The OCI adapter owns and uses a separate value:

  oci.py:846  OciAdapter.__init__(..., mounts=(), ...)
  oci.py:861  self.mounts = tuple(mounts)
  oci.py:898  OciAdapter.start(request)
  oci.py:967  run_vector(..., mounts=self.mounts, ...)

`_mounts` validates containment and writability but does not require an input
mount, require the authenticated source, or require target `/input`.
Therefore authorization of root A says nothing about whether the runtime
mounts A, B, nothing, or an allowed source at `/inputs`.
