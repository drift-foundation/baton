  THE REQUIREMENT IS DERIVED, NOT OPTIONAL, and that distinction is the
  design. `inputs=None` is reachable only when the attempt records no
  input digest -- meaning this manager has nothing a root could be bound
  to and no root to expose. Every real delivery is offered and claimed
