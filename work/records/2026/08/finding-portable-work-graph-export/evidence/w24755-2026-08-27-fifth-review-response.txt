  _export_scope_document validated each bound with _export_instant and

  caller spelled. 2026-01-01T01:00:00+01:00 and 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z

  THE OPERAND PATH IS UNTOUCHED and still accepts every legal spelling —
  it normalizes before building the scope, so the rule it meets here is
  already satisfied. A canonical rule that leaked into the operand path
  would turn a determinism guarantee into a usability regression, so
