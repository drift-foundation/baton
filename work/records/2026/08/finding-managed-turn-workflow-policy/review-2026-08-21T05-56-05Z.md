same-binary/config/participant allow rule as `extra` only when its pattern has
exactly one element after the participant prefix. Codex prefix rules may carry
additional operands, so this policy is accepted:

```text
<all 30 generated exact rules>
prefix_rule(pattern=["/opt/baton/bin/baton", "--config",
  "/srv/baton/baton.json", "--participant", "baton.codex", "regen",
  "op-id=authorized-extra"], decision="allow")
```

Direct reproduction against the changed module returned
`missing=[]`, `broad=[]`, `extra=[]`, and `satisfied=true`. The added rule can
authorize the excluded `regen` invocation with that operand, contrary to the
confirmed rule that the nominated participant policy is the exact approved set
and that excluded deployment mutations fail closed. The same hole applies to
unknown/future verbs and operand-qualified read rules.

Change the same-identity extra-capability test to inspect every allow pattern
that has a verb after the participant prefix, not only patterns of length
`prefix.length + 1`. Add regressions for at least an excluded verb plus an
operand and an unknown verb plus an operand; the exact 30 generated rules must
remain accepted.
