## [P1] An exact retry collides solely because the manager's clock advanced

`_ask` computes `requested_at` and `deadline_at` before replay and includes the
derived absolute deadline in the operation signature
(`interrogation.py:193-202`). Calling the same public operation with the same
operation ID and caller operands at a later instant therefore produces a new
signature and `operation-collision`, rather than replaying the journalled
request without asking the adapter again. Wall time is not a caller operand;
this makes effectively-once identity fail across the ordinary restart the
durable journal exists to survive.

The additive
`test_an_exact_retry_does_not_collide_with_the_managers_new_clock` at
`tests/manager/test_interrogation.py:334` advances only the injected clock. It
errors with `operation-collision`. Build the signature from stable caller and
durable binding operands (including the requested duration), while committing
and replaying the first manager-observed absolute deadline as the operation's
result.

## [P1] A successful probe's observation is transient and lost on replay

`probe` passes the adapter observation only as an argument to
`settle_interrogation`; the `interrogations` row has no observation column, and
`_view` receives no observation when reconstructing a row
(`interrogation.py:260-263, 474-490`). Consequently the fresh call reports the
promised state/activity/diagnostics, while an exact replay, lookup, list, or
manager restart reports only `outcome: observed` and silently omits the reading
that makes a probe useful.

The additive `test_a_restart_reads_a_probe_observation_not_only_its_outcome`
at `test_interrogation.py:666` errors because `observation` is absent after
reopen. Persist the closed typed observation atomically with the `observed`
transition and restore it on every durable view; make the schema reject an
`observed` probe without its observation and observations on other outcomes.

## [P1] The closed adapter shape does not type the probe observation

`boundaries.alternative` closes member names but intentionally does not own
their values. Unlike `reconcile_agent_session`, the probe path performs no
subsequent validation of `state`, `last_activity_at`, or `diagnostics` before
returning them. A runtime-axis state such as `running` therefore crosses as an
agent-session state, collapsing two vocabularies this Work explicitly keeps
distinct.

The additive `test_a_probe_state_is_owned_as_the_frozen_session_vocabulary` at
`test_interrogation.py:400` fails because no refusal is raised. Own `state`
against the frozen session vocabulary, `last_activity_at` as an instant, and
`diagnostics` as the contract's bounded document/value shape before any value
is returned or persisted.
