A NEW W6629 DEFECT SURFACED WHILE THIS WORK RAN, AND IT IS MINE

  `tests.manager.test_intake.IndependentContractReview.
  test_a_later_policy_can_replace_part_of_a_grouped_decision` was added by
  W6629's review at 09:13, after I passed that Work back, and it errors.

  It is a real defect in the grouping logic I wrote for W6629's [P1] on
  journal authentication. `retentions_of` groups persisted decisions by their
  `retain_operation_id` and reconstructs the command each group came from. When
  a LATER policy replaces the decision for one artifact of a group, the
  remaining row is a fragment of the earlier command -- and reconstructing from
  that fragment derives an operation the journal does not carry, so a perfectly
  legitimate partial replacement reads as a forged row.

  W6629 is routed to the reviewer and this Work touches no file of it, so it is
  reported rather than fixed here. It is the next thing to correct when W6629
  routes back.
