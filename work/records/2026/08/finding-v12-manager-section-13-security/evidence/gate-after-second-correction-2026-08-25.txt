WHAT THE RE-AUDIT FOUND, which is the part worth reading

  The re-review asked for a re-audit of prose-only classifications whose
  reasons might describe a narrower internal caller than the public path. Two
  were named. A THIRD was found, and it was not a wording defect:

    record_inquiry_answer

  Its durable-sweep entry and its public entry both said the answer rode the
  same journalled signature `_ask` walks. It does not. An answer arrives at
  its own boundary long after the request, and that function writes through a
  direct `UPDATE ... SET answer = ?` rather than through `transact` — so
  `_record`'s walk never ran on it, and an answer body carrying a live bearer
  reached durable storage with no §13 guard at all.

  Guarded at its own boundary now, with three regressions in
  `ARecordedAnswerIsWalkedAtItsOwnBoundary`: a live bearer refused with the
  column still NULL and the outcome not `answered`; a member named for a
  secret refused whether or not one is held; and an ordinary answer recorded
  unchanged, so the guard refuses a leak and nothing else.

  `request_freeze`'s reason was corrected too — it answers with a request
  document over owned identities, not "with what `record_frozen_result`
  walked".
