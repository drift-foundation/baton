`category`, and `code` explicitly so the later reader can know what the record
decided. `_refused_session_record` owns the shape and compares attempt,
assignment, runtime, posture, epoch and provider session, but never checks
those verdict members. It then digests the entire result, and that digest is
the authorization sent through `destroy_refused_session`.

The additive
`test_a_record_that_no_longer_says_unsupported_version_authorizes_nothing`
commits a genuine refusal, preserves the exact member set and all six compared
identities, changes only `decision` to `accepted`, and calls cleanup. The test
fails because no `ContractRefusal` is raised: the custodian is invoked and the
attempt settles.

At the receiving authorization boundary, require the exact closed verdict
`decision == "unsupported-version"`, `category == "refused"`, and
`code == "unsupported-version"` before computing the authorization digest.
Also validate that pinned and answered versions remain typed and unequal and
that the retained `profile_digest` agrees with the persisted session. This
must operate on the retained committed evidence so exact replay still works
after profile certification is withdrawn.
