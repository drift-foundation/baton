`evidence/w32648-review-runtime-binding-2026-08-29.txt`.

`runtime.start-failed`, and compare the retained result to the current fixed
attempt/start/runtime facts before crossing the adapter. The journal result
remains the source of the failure identity and digest; a disagreement is an
integrity failure, not a reason to recompose authorization.
