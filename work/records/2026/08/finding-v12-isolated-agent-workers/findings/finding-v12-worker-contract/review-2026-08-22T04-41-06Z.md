## Result

**Signed off.** No cross-contract defect remains.

- The three compatible families freeze as
  `urn:baton:worker-control:1.0`, `urn:baton:agent-session:1.0`, and
  `urn:baton:worker-conformance:1.0`. Unknown major versions and
  unnegotiated capabilities fail closed throughout.
- W1440 and W1441 import W1439's shared definitions byte-identically. They do
  not mint a second Work identity, assignment generation, settlement ledger,
  publication authority or runtime lifecycle.
- The outer contract owns assignment, fencing, runtime control and artifact
  exchange. The agent contract owns only normalized sessions, turns,
  permission/tool exchange, cancellation and session events. Provider and
  runtime details remain diagnostics.
- W1439 freezes 17 control message kinds, 31 typed error pairs and 10 manifest
  or receipt schemas. W1440 freezes eight turn outcomes, nine session states,
  10 normalized event kinds and four approval families. W1441 mechanically
  binds both vocabularies into 68 obligations and 107 cases, selecting 106 for
  local and 107 for remote profiles.
- The current official Codex App Server documentation still exposes explicit
  turn interruption, streamed turn/item events and server-initiated approvals,
  while describing WebSocket transport and `process/*` as experimental. The
  captured Codex profile correctly sets `experimentalApi` false and keeps
  those experimental surfaces out of portable Baton semantics.
- All provider-free executable gates pass: 12 W1439 tests, 56 W1440 tests and
  73 W1441 tests, 141 total. Cross-family generation also confirms identical
  shared definitions and the expected local/remote case selections.

## Acceptance boundary

The parent record's six M1 freeze conditions are met. This sign-off approves
the design family and its machine-readable examples for the approving
endpoint's decision. It does not authorize implementation and does not certify
any authority, worker runtime, ACP relay or provider adapter.
