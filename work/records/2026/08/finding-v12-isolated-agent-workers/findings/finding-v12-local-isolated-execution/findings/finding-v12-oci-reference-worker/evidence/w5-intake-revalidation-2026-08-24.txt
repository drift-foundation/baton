OPEN TOPOLOGY RULING
OCI mounts are fixed when a container is created. A single container cannot
both expose no writable workspace during consent and later acquire the
activated execution workspace without being replaced or having that
capability pre-mounted. Pre-mounting violates the frozen timing rule; reusing
the consent session violates agent-session 1.0. Recommend a consent OCI
runtime followed by a distinct execution OCI runtime under the same
runtime_attempt_id, with positive consent teardown before activation.
