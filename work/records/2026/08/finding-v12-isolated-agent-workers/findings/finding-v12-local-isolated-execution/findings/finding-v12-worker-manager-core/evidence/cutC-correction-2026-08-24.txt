2. [P1] THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST IS THE ONE I WROTE MYSELF

Cut B established `_durable_text` and applied it at six sites, and the evidence
for that round says so at length. Cut C then declared a LOCAL `_text` one file
over -- weaker, accepting lone surrogates, and treating any nonempty string as an
instant.

That is the seventh time this campaign a rule has been applied at one of N sites,
and the first where the other site was mine and three days old. Writing the rule
down did not stop me; having it in a different module did not stop me. There is
no local rule now: both are imported, so there is one of each.

AND THE INSTANT IS A SECOND PROPERTY, not a stronger version of the first.
Encodable text keeps a value out of SQLite's way; the frozen grammar keeps it out
of a COMPARISON's way, because a fixed-width lexicographic instant sorts exactly
as it orders in time and arbitrary text sorts too. The review names them as
separate and they are separate rules.

THE CLOCK IS HELD TO BOTH NOW. Cut B proved the manager's instant source was
storable; every deadline this manager compares is derived from it, so storable
and ORDERABLE were two properties there as well.
