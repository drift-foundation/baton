`readPolicy` correctly recognises a comment at the top level, indented at the
start of a line, or after a complete rule. Inside a construct, however,
`matchingParen` retains the comment and `splitTopLevel` sends it into
`decompose` as part of the next operand. The operand is therefore unreadable
even though the evaluator discards the comment and loads the exact rule.

hidden, but dispatcher startup rejects a correct policy and asks the operator
to regenerate evaluator-valid text. It also narrows the record's promise that
a comment is accounted for “wherever it sits” without recording that narrower
boundary.
