## P1 — the supervision window is reusable data, not a per-turn identity

The prior review required one stable manager/relay-owned identity component
per supervised turn, allocated or validated atomically within the session
epoch. The correction instead adds caller-supplied `startedAt` and
`deadlineAt` values to the digest while retaining `promptDigest` as the value
that disambiguates reuse of that window.

Nothing allocates the window as a turn identity, records that allocation, or
enforces uniqueness within the epoch. The database has no corresponding
constraint, and the new test deliberately proves that the same window may be
reused when the prompt differs. Consequently a changed prompt under the same
manager window silently mints another turn rather than colliding as changed
operands for one supervised act. Conversely, two distinct same-prompt acts
are distinguishable only when their timestamp fields happen to differ. This
fixes the sampled timestamps without supplying the per-turn identity the
finding called for.

Introduce an explicit manager-owned turn token/ordinal allocated at the
supervision boundary and durably bound to the epoch, or atomically enforce and
bind an equivalent window identity. Prompt bytes belong in the effective
signature; they cannot be the fallback that decides whether one reusable
window means one act or two. Preserve same-prompt later-turn separation,
exact replay, changed-operand collision, and the deliberate exclusion of the
observed `endedAt`. Revalidate the older same-window/different-prompt fixture
against the corrected identity contract rather than treating test setup as a
product ruling.
