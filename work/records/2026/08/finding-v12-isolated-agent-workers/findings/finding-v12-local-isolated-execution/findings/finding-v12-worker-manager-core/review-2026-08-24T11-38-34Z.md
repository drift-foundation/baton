## Accepted

AuthorityPort now refuses every non-callable session operation at construction.
The named offer and time operands share Cut B's durable validators. Exact

## P1 — six text identities remain outside the shared rule

`submit_claim` and `settle_claim` query by unproved `offer_id`, while
`expire_overdue` sends its optional `work_id` directly to SQLite. A lone
surrogate on each path escapes as raw `UnicodeEncodeError`.

`certify_profile` validates `profile_digest` but not `kind` or `name`.
Integers are canonicalized, interpolated into durable metadata keys, and
committed. Canonicalizability does not establish exact durable text.

The injected `claim_signature` derivation is checked for callability, but its
answer is not checked. Returning `None` reaches the schema-v4 CHECK during
acceptance and escapes as raw `sqlite3.IntegrityError`. This answer becomes a
frozen TEXT identity and must pass the shared durable-text rule before the
acceptance transaction opens.

Apply the shared rule at the owning common boundaries, not only at the
individual witnesses: lookup identity before `_offer_row`, optional filtered
Work identity before the expiry query, both certification key parts before key
composition, and the signature answer before persistence.

## P1 — positive exact integers can still escape deadline arithmetic

`ttl_seconds=10 ** 100` satisfies the new positive-exact-integer test, performs
the authority reads, and then escapes from `timedelta` as `OverflowError`.
Prove both duration representation and clock-plus-duration representation in
the frozen deadline domain before authority reads or entropy, with a closed
contract refusal outside that domain.
