1. WHAT THIS SLICE IS

  "Every turn has a manager deadline. Turn outcome is selected only from the
   closed eight-value vocabulary and only from a terminal provider fact,
   policy failure, deadline or transport death. It gates but never chooses
   worker disposition."

Schema 9 -> 10 adds `turns`, with `deadline_at` NOT NULL and the derived
`permitted` set stored beside the outcome.


2. SELECTED, NEVER INFERRED

The ACP boundary's §5.4 lists what an outcome is NOT derived from: silence,
transport closure, an empty update stream, a tool call's own status, agent
prose, and reachability at any layer. Each is a guess somebody could defend,
which is why the section exists.

So `selectTurnOutcome` takes the evidence a relay actually holds and REFUSES
when none of it names an outcome. A turn that ended with no terminal fact, no
policy failure, no transport death and before its deadline is a turn nobody
can name an outcome for, and the alternative to refusing is guessing.

THE ORDER OF PRECEDENCE IS AN ARGUMENT, not a convenience:

  - a §4 violation ends the turn WHERE IT HAPPENS, so it outranks anything
    that arrives afterwards, including a terminal fact;
  - a terminal provider fact outranks an elapsed deadline, because the fact
    ARRIVED;
  - transport death outranks the deadline for the reason it is a separate
    outcome at all: the epoch is gone, which is more than "nothing has come
    back yet".

The ACP `stopReason` mapping is EXACT — five reasons, and an unknown one is
refused rather than rounded to the nearest. A JSON-RPC error response is
`agent-failed` and its payload is never parsed to select a different outcome.


3. GATES, NEVER CHOOSES

The worker declares its disposition; the outcome decides only whether such a
declaration may be accepted. The acceptance table is transcribed verbatim and
DRIVEN EXHAUSTIVELY — all eight rows, and both directions of the permitted
set — because a closed vocabulary tested at three of eight points is a closed
vocabulary nobody has checked.

`cancelled` and `policy-failed` permit NOTHING because the assignment is
already ended and the material goes to quarantine. `timeout` and
`transport-lost` permit nothing because they are the honest outcomes that say
the relay does not know, and a disposition accepted on "I do not know" is
§5.4's inference wearing a different hat. Those two are also the only two that
are not `conclusive`.

The permitted set is stored beside the turn rather than re-derived on read, so
a later reader sees THE GATE THAT WAS APPLIED rather than one from a table
that may have moved since.

is not an ordering.

5. REGRESSIONS — 13, in a new suite

The two tables asserted against the closed vocabulary and each other, so an
outcome without a gate or without a conclusiveness is caught rather than
defaulted; both mappings driven for EVERY key plus unknown values; no evidence
naming nothing; a turn ending before its deadline with nothing; each of the
deadline, transport death and policy failure naming exactly one; the
precedence order in all three of its pairs; the gate applied and not chosen,
in both directions; all four permit-nothing outcomes crossed with all three
dispositions; the stored gate being what a later reader sees; the deadline
required in all three of its timestamps; a turn refused outside a session; and
the turn identity varying with epoch, posture and prompt.
