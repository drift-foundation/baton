## Findings

### [P1] An assignment can name a different authority and still be recorded

`AuthorityPort._assignment` owns the returned `authority_uuid` as durable text
and relates Work and participant, but it never compares that authority with the
authority frozen on the offer. Both `submit_claim` and the late-commit branch of
`settle_claim` therefore accept a well-shaped assignment from authority
`ffffffffffffffffffffffffffffffff` for an offer issued from authority
`0000000000000000000000000000000a`, advance the offer to `claimed`, and record
the foreign assignment's generation. The four-part assignment identity is not
owned if one of its relationship fields is only shaped.

Retain both additive regressions in `test_offers.py`. The shared correction
belongs in `_assignment`: both claim paths must supply and compare the offer's
owned authority as well as its Work and participant before `_record_claim`
writes anything. Extend the inventory's stated relationship rules and probes so
this relation cannot disappear while the type check remains.

### [P1] The inventory still treats semantic fields as container presence

The green 51-method inventory gate does not enumerate a retirement record's
`reason` and `disposition`; origin tracking stops at
`bound = answer.get("record") or {}`. `AuthorityPort.settle_operation` checks
only that both names exist. An integer reason is then adopted as the terminal
decision and the offer settles instead of refusing at the injected crossing.

The adopted side has the same gap. `Column.members` is a tuple of required
names, so an operation row containing a sealed refusal with integer `category`
passes `boundaries.row`. Replay reaches `ContractRefusal` with that integer and
escapes as `AssertionError`. A closed member set is not a field contract here
any more than it was for the claim answer in the preceding review.

Retain the retirement and sealed-refusal regressions. Extend the centralized
universe to semantic fields consumed through aliases/boolean expressions and
to the fields of adopted structured columns. Give each field one type/value or
relationship owner and one probe. The sealed refusal must establish the closed
category/code pairing, text message, and durable marker at its one adopted
crossing; the retirement must own the text it records and branches on.
