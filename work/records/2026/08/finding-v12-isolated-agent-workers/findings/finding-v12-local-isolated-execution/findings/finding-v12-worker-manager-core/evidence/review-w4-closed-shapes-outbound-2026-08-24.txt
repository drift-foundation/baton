Focused behavior
----------------

* A session participant containing a lone surrogate is accepted by
  AuthorityPort construction. The inventory discovers capability CALLS only,
  not the bound participant value.
* A non-callable mint_bearer reaches a raw TypeError after project_work,
  certification, expiry processing and slot_holder. The inventory deliberately
  removes mint_bearer from caller entries while no constructor types it.
* A projection whose authority_uuid is integer 7 is accepted and offer issuance
  succeeds. Exact member sets and exact POD do not establish the semantic type
  of an identity that is then put in the operation signature and TEXT column.
* A claim answer naming another participant is accepted and recorded. A claim
  answer whose generation is `not-a-generation` escapes as
  sqlite3.IntegrityError at offers.claim_generation after the authority has
  already answered the claim.
* receiving_entries iterates positional and keyword-only arguments but not
  vararg/kwarg. Nine public documents.py constructors with **members are absent
  from the claimed every-public-parameter universe.
* _operation_row owns and JSON-checks a refusal column, then replay calls public
  revive_refusal, which invokes boundaries.adopted and boundaries.document on
  that same value again. Patching the second adoption makes an exact durable
  refusal replay fail, proving the duplicate crossing.
