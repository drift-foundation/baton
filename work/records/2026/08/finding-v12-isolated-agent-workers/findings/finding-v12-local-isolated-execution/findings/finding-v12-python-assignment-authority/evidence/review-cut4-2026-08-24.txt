Finding 1 — result_id is neither parsed as an opaque ID nor bound immutably
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
`Authority.publish` validates `proposal_id` with `check_opaque_id`, but includes
`result_id` in the digest mapping and therefore applies only `check_text` to it.
An independent probe published and read back:

  result_id="bad result id"

A second probe published these two proposals successfully:

  [('result-1', 'sha256:first'), ('result-1', 'sha256:other')]

Thus one purported immutable-result identity can durably name contradictory
content. W151 section 4 defines `result_id` as an opaque immutable-result
identifier with a required content digest and assignment_ref. Consistent reuse
may be valid, but reuse must preserve both its digest and full assignment
identity.

Finding 2 — canonical_target is an untyped special policy
---------------------------------------------------------
`set_policy` accepts arbitrary JSON and `canonical_target` returns the value

target escaped as:

or integration can reach SQLite. Generic JSON policy storage need not change.
