2. THE FINDING, AND WHY MY OWN CORRECTION MISSED IT

Last round I split the inbound route out of the client-call boundary and
wrote that "direction answers two different questions". Then I built the
outbound allow list as `REQUIRED_AGENT_METHODS + OPTIONAL_AGENT_METHODS` —
all five required plus three — so it still admitted `session/update` in the
reverse direction. The two closed directional surfaces I had just separated
overlapped on the exact name the separation was about.

The mistake is a misreading of section 2.3. Its five-member baseline says what
an ENDPOINT must present across both directions; it is not a relay-outbound
list. I used it as one.

REVALIDATED AGAINST THE PINNED SDK RATHER THAN THE PROSE, since that is now
twice this slice's lesson. `@agentclientprotocol/sdk` 1.3.0's
`dist/schema/index.js`:

  CLIENT_METHODS  session_update: "session/update"   (with the fs/*,
                  terminal/*, mcp/* and elicitation/* names)
  AGENT_METHODS   initialize, session/new, session/prompt, session/cancel,
                  and all three optional names — session/set_mode,
                  session/set_config_option, session/close

So the split is four relay-origin required plus three relay-origin optional.


3. THE CHANGE

`REQUIRED_AGENT_METHODS` stays at FIVE and is still what handshake
capability validation requires — nothing about the endpoint's obligation
changed.

`KNOWN_AGENT_SURFACE` is renamed `RELAY_OUTBOUND_SURFACE`, on the review's
explicit authority, so the direction is in the name rather than in a comment.
It is DERIVED rather than transcribed:

  RELAY_OUTBOUND_SURFACE =
      REQUIRED_AGENT_METHODS minus AGENT_ORIGIN_METHODS,
      plus OPTIONAL_AGENT_METHODS

so the two directional lists cannot drift apart under a later edit to either.
Seven names. `checkOutboundMethod("session/update")` refuses
`refused.capability`; `routeAgentOriginCall("session/update")` still returns
it.

5. REGRESSIONS — 21 (20 before, including the reviewer's one)

One of mine: the two directional surfaces PARTITION the required five. Not
merely disjoint — between them they account for every required name, so a
member cannot be dropped from both and go unnoticed, and no agent-origin name
can appear that is not part of the baseline. The outbound length is asserted
as four plus three by arithmetic over the three lists rather than as a
literal, so a change to any one of them has to be deliberate.

The outbound pass-through case was migrated on the review's authority: the
handshake requirement is still asserted at five, the optional three are
asserted by name, and the exhaustive pass-through now runs over the seven
relay-outbound names — the same assertion this case already made over the set
it then had.
