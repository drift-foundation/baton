1. AN EFFECT OUTSIDE THE TRANSACTION IS NOT PART OF THE ACT

  `_attach` committed the runtime identity and the operation result, and THEN
  called `observe(... running)`. A fault between them left a committed
  attachment whose exact retry replayed the recorded answer without running the
  action -- so it answered `attached` forever while the durable axis still said
  `start-requested`.

  The observation is inside the journalled transaction now, which is what the
  observation savepoint was built for: it is the same all-or-nothing boundary at
  either depth, where a second BEGIN would refuse. Effectively-once means the
  retry reproduces the first act's WHOLE effect, and an effect outside the
  transaction is not part of it.

  A CHANGE TO THE REVIEWER'S RETAINED TEST, reported rather than made quietly.
  The review offered two remedies and recommended this one; under it, the
  crash in that test commits NOTHING, so the two premise assertions -- that the
  attachment is committed and the axis unmoved -- describe a state the fix makes
  impossible. I changed those two lines to assert what atomicity produces (no
  attachment at all) and left everything after the store close/reopen exactly as
  the review wrote it, because that is the property the case exists for: after a
  crash and a reopen, reconciliation ends `attached` with the axis `running`.

  The stronger outcome is that there is no window to repair. If the review
  prefers the other remedy -- a committed attachment that a later replay heals --
  I will implement that instead; I have not assumed the choice is mine.

2. A FENCE FOR ANOTHER GENERATION IS NOT THIS CANCELLATION

  `AuthorityPort.cancel` related the authority, the Work and the participant and
  left the GENERATION only shape-checked. So a fence of generation 2 was
  accepted for an attempt expecting generation 1, and the provider agent and the
  runtime were then ordered with no evidence that THIS attempt's generation had
  been fenced -- which is the one fact the whole fence-then-stop ordering rests
  on.

  The returned assignment is compared to the exact expected assignment on all
  four members before either downstream boundary can run. A fence is an act
  about ONE generation; three quarters of a match is not that act.
