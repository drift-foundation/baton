3. [P1] REPRESENTABILITY IS A THIRD PROPERTY

`ttl_seconds = 10 ** 100` is an integer and is positive and is not a duration
this domain can express -- it escaped `timedelta` as `OverflowError` after the
authority reads had already happened.

It is a property of the SUM rather than of either operand: a small duration added
to a late instant leaves the domain just as surely. And the deadline is computed
BEFORE the authority is read at all now, because the clock is this manager's own
and nothing about computing a deadline needs the authority -- which makes "before
reads or entropy" true rather than nearly true.
