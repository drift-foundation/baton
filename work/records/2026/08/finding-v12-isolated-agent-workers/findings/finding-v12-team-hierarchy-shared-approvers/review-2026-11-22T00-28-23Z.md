1. **Project binding.** Scenario: one team owns two repositories, while two teams collaborate on one repository. Must both be representable now? 2. **Who may satisfy lifecycle conditions and change a Job plan?** Scenario: independent review accepts a plan, implementation discovers a missing test phase, or the human answers an escalated design question. Options: designated scoped role authorities with automatic recorded transitions, or human approval for every plan change. This settles the missing authorizer rather than asking again whether automatic transitions are wanted.

3. **Cross-project intake and answer authority.** Scenario: A files a defect with B and B asks A a clarification while A's feature is blocked. Options: destination-granted intake/answer capabilities resolved from configured roles, or authenticated open intake with destination triage. Recommend explicit destination-scoped intake grants and a destination-role resolver for answers, with a deployment-wide grant available where open internal filing is wanted. Choose this before cross-scope mutation implementation, not before a read-only reader.

4. **Publishing reusable templates versus changing one Job.** Scenario: a defect Job adds a useful phase and proposes a reusable improved template. Start with the confirmed feature/planning and defect lifecycles; no exhaustive type catalogue or generic editor is needed before the first bounded slice.

5. **When may review fall back to the same model family?** Scenario: the alternative family is busy, unavailable, or unknown. Options: immediate recorded fallback when no eligible alternate is available, or a bounded preference wait followed by recorded fallback. Recommend deployment-configured bounded wait; use observed unavailable/no eligible configured lane, never treat unknown as proven unavailable; after the configured wait, record the reason and select a distinct principal/context with the same independence checks.
