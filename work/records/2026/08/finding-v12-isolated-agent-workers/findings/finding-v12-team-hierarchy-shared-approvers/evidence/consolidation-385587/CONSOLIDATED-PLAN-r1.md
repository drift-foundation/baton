## 3. Workflow model: objectives, phases, teams and obligations

### Durable Job and execution mapping

**Confirmed:** a user-facing Job is a durable objective containing conversation, decisions, deliverables and acceptance. Every Job has phases appropriate to its type. A child Job has its own bounded outcome and phases; nesting a phase does not create a child Job. Containment and cross-Job prerequisites remain distinct relations. Scheduler state is not lifecycle phase.

**Proposed compatibility mapping:** retain current Work identifiers as the durable objective anchor; link existing execution Job identifiers to the selected phase/activity; preserve stage and attempt identities as execution pipeline/invocation evidence. Represent the instantiated phase plan separately from queued/active/block/parked and from current execution stages. This is a proposal for an explicit reviewed contract, not a silent rename or 1:1 migration. Exact completion-authority and project-binding questions are U1/U2 below.

The feature lifecycle includes design/discussion, plan creation, independent plan review, implementation, verification/review and human integration/acceptance. Appropriate entry points follow an approved template/plan; not every objective must start with design. Defect phases use the owner's exact sequence above. Verification also occurs throughout as needed; a phase name does not prohibit earlier experiments/regressions. A planning execution finishing supplies a plan, not overall feature closure. Review corrections, questions and discovered defects preserve context/evidence and follow explicitly authorized transitions.

### Templates, plans and authority

Each Job starts from an identified immutable template. Its plan may evolve, but **every** phase-plan change is proposed, reviewed as appropriate, explicitly approved by Slawomir and recorded before application. Completed evidence and required acceptance remain. No agent/reviewer can approve the mutation instead. Publishing a new reusable template is also owner-approved and creates a new identity/version with provenance; it never changes existing bindings. Ordinary instantiation and progression through already approved conditions are automatic and are not new publication/plan changes.

Routine answers never silently change specification, acceptance or phase plan. A separate discovered defect becomes its own objective; only explicit dependencies hold the affected phase. Approval of a recovery, a phase-plan change, a template publication and a pool replacement are distinct authorities: one decision must not imply the others unless its recorded scope explicitly includes them.

### Team interface and return delivery

A team is a shared working-access and durable knowledge domain; access is still role-specific. Knowledge survives Jobs, member/model replacement and session loss. A session/context is a separately protected reuse artifact, not the team's durable memory. Members do not enter another team's coding sessions. An eligible shared-pool worker may serve another team on a separately bounded assignment; that is not cross-team session/access sharing.

Any internal team may file a defect, feature request or question at another team's public interface, without learning its internal phases or assignees. Identity validation and explicit intake authority implement this open policy; there is no per-team consent gate. The destination owns triage. Filing creates no execution, access grant, containment or dependency by implication.

Each exchange retains the submitting member, originating Job and phase, stable request/conversation identity and specific return address. A response may provide a subsequent reply-to without replacing original lineage. The team receiving a response resolves its own member availability and fallback: member → originating-project responsible role → originating-team responsible role → human operator Slawomir. The responding team does not manage the caller's availability. Missing/unavailable intermediate members cannot lose the obligation; there must remain one accountable destination, never duplicate competing ownership. Concrete role bindings/timing/resolution semantics remain U2/U3.

An action-required response stays an obligation until explicitly resolved. Delivery, seen, notification or a transport acknowledgment is not resolution. Informational messages need not create recursive acknowledgment obligations. Shared internal visibility includes work, findings, decisions and dependency chains, without granting execution/mutation, repository/credential access or session sharing. The earlier metadata-only/body-scoped proposal is not a selected default.

### Dependencies and retained continuation

Containment is a tree; prerequisite relationships are separate. Clarification must remain independently runnable even when its originating feature waits for another team's fix. Answering it satisfies the question, not the fix or feature. Response, accepted implementation, integration and usable revision have distinct satisfaction evidence. Technical review, termination, file existence or private retained bytes cannot substitute for the exact accepted usable base where required.

Satisfaction changes eligibility; the scheduler subsequently admits automatically under current ownership, cancellation, deadline, capacity and input checks. It does not introduce a human “go” gate. Rejection/cancellation, wrong/superseded candidate, unknown acceptance or incompatible restoration retain an explicit unresolved condition. Never resurrect a cancelled execution or silently graft its checkpoint onto another base. Replacement/recovery needs the explicit authorized identity and preserved lineage. Existing admitted deadlines are not paused/reset by this plan.

## 5. Shared execution pools: separate from workflow responsibility

Pools represent subscriptions/accounts/plans such as codex-1, codex-2 or claude-1. Each can supply multiple concurrent agents across eligible teams/projects and all roles. Team/project responsibility, participant authority, repository access, durable knowledge and Job/phase identity remain separate from the provider/model/pool supplying execution. Compatible context affinity is a preference, not permanent team reservation. Incompatible provider context is not transplanted; use an attributable durable checkpoint/handoff.

Initially pools have no Baton-imposed per-pool concurrency cap. This does not mean unlimited provider/host resources or removal of existing ownership, isolation, independent-review and per-execution constraints. Gather attributable concurrency, provider refusal/throttle, queue size/oldest wait, waiting/completion and context-reuse observations. Queue admission continues during trouble; it does not itself claim capacity or launch work. No queue-size policy, automatic load balancing or credit tuning is selected.

| Situation | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Preferred alternate review family busy | Wait. Elapsed time alone never authorizes same-model fallback. Operator may explicitly reassign. |
| Transient busy/try-later provider refusal | Pause the affected work initially, investigate shared-pool trouble, allow roughly 1–2 minutes before further action. Exact observation/recheck mechanics remain U4. No immediate automatic failover or queue-admission stop. |
| Pool unavailable/exhausted/down | Effective new-start capacity zero; preserve configured capacity, assignments and evidence. This does not prove active processes stopped. Report/analyze alternatives; operator selects replacement. |
| Operator lowers a cap or sets zero | Accept queued assignments; admit new starts only below the limit, none at zero. Healthy active agents always finish; never kill them just to meet the cap. Explicit cancellation/deadlines are separate. |
| Operator moves one responsibility | Preserve exact Job/phase/task/candidate/instructions/evidence and role authority. Compatible context or durable handoff; replacement waits for cessation/fencing where a prior execution exists. No implicit move of other work. |
| Operator redirects a pool allocation | Move affected queued work and future assignments; healthy running agents finish in original pool with original attribution. Exceptional executions retain their separate recovery gate. |
| Original provider recovers | No automatic failback. Redirect persists until the operator explicitly changes it. |

Owner-selected temporary throttling is in scope and independent of the initial uncapped default. There is no automatic tuning. Same-vendor or weaker standby pools are permitted when selected; same-model review remains an explicitly recorded separate-identity/context fallback, never a waiver of independence. Pool switching is **never automatic**. This later rule constrains the older actual-unavailability fallback permission: it is not permission to autonomously pick a new supplying pool.

Redirect scope precedence is **project > team > global**, using the most-specific applicable rule; global effect is explicit. Redirects are visible, attributable and persistent. They change supply routing, not team authority or phase plan. Proposed validation rejects ambiguous/cyclic configurations rather than guessing. An individual reassignment remains distinct from persistent scoped redirection; recording its target must not silently install a global or future-assignment rule. Race-safe queue movement versus admission must choose one execution owner and retain the winner's attribution. These are correctness obligations, not invitations to design advanced scheduling.
