## Recovery guarantees: verified faithful

- **Trigger.** The trigger is a terminal `verification-failed` ending with provider status 0 and a validated retained candidate. Runtime and reservation settlement come from their owners. A candidate that fails validation is reported, never treated as recovery input. The stable failure key prevents a duplicate ending from creating a second analysis or owner item. This matches the addendum's D1 and M385601.
- **Analysis authority.** The analysis role is dedicated and configured, not tied to a model. It may only inspect evidence and author its report. It cannot change the candidate, verifier, execution state or recovery authorization, and the host records all state transitions. This matches M385599.
- **Finite analysis-failure endpoint.** On analysis failure, partial evidence is kept and Slawomir is notified directly. There is no retry or recursive analysis, and the original failure and the failed analysis stay distinct. This matches M385600.
- **Owner decision.** Slawomir alone decides.
  - The decision binds the exact failure, report and candidate digests, the original and corrected verifier inputs and execution context, the verification/review-only scope and the profile.
  - A generic "retry", an acknowledgment, a review approval or a recommendation cannot authorize a successor.
  - Any phase-plan change must be named explicitly in the approval.
  - Changed inputs require a new decision.
  - This matches M385601, M385556 and addendum D1.
- **Successor.** The successor is new, linked and manager-owned, and is not a fresh attempt. The contract confirms that `_composed` copies the prior stages, which justifies a new typed admission. No implementation stage or coding prompt can run.
  - It materializes an isolated workspace from the exact retained bytes and keeps the original custody immutable.
  - Verifier-mutated source is never published as a candidate.
  - Review receives the same bound candidate.
  - This matches M385601.
- **Renewed failure.** One new analysis and one new owner item follow, never an automatic further successor, and every round needs a new decision. This matches PLAN M385598 and my previous low note.
- **Replay, restart and safety.**
  - Intent is persisted before effects. Exact retry replays; a changed operand refuses.
  - Lost replies never cause blind redispatch, and there is no pretended cross-store atomicity, per DB-3–DB-5.
  - Cancellation blocks admission and nothing is revived. Unknown cessation holds.
  - Old deadlines are never reset, and the original failure stays visible after recovery.
  - Faults are injected at every listed cut, and message deduplication is not claimed as "exactly once".
  - This is consistent with REC-1–REC-6 and LIM-5.
- **Narrow seam.** No scheduler, phase engine, team graph, pool framework or launcher is introduced. W385331 is not assumed. W16830 is kept to its separate attestation scope.

## Findings

**E1 (high; authority): the new finite profiles need owner publication approval.**
- **What the contract says.** Section 5 proposes two NEW reusable profiles, one for analysis and one for the verification/review successor. It then says the coordinator pins them and that they are "not a new product decision or a request for another owner approval". The manifest repeats "no new owner-approval gate".
- **Why that is wrong.**
  - M385557: every new reusable template requires Slawomir's explicit approval.
  - Accepted plan r2, item 2-C and section 8: a new reusable execution profile is under the same publication gate.
  - K review `04-17-25Z`, minor 4, as answered in r2 section 9: this includes profiles needed by acceptance fixtures.
  - LIM-5: the existing `documentation/1` values were themselves an explicit owner selection.
  - M385601: execution-limit fields must be pinned with "unresolved product semantics escalated rather than invented".
- **What does not replace it.** The per-recovery owner decision "naming the profile" in section 2 is a use of a profile, not its publication.
- **Required change.** Present the two profiles to Slawomir for publication approval with values, clock origins and correction policy. Record the approval in FINDING and give each profile an identity and digest before any dependent coding. Coordinator pinning may follow that approval but cannot replace it. Strike the "no owner approval" sentence and the manifest's `remaining` claim.

**E2 (medium; unlisted real choice): the successor's clock can expire between a passed verification and a completed review.**
- **The arithmetic.** The proposed successor Job has a 9960 s limit and two 4800 s stages. Under LIM-5, Job time starts at accepted creation and includes queue time, so the stages consume 9600 s. Only 360 s remain for queueing and the review handoff.
- **The conflict.** M385559 requires waiting when the preferred alternate review family is busy, with no deadline reset. A busy reviewer can therefore expire a successor whose verification already passed.
- **What is unspecified.** The contract does not say what that ending is. It is not `verification-failed`, so the 1-A trigger does not apply. It is not a review outcome either. Two related structural choices are also open:
  - whether review should be a separate linked admission with its own clock, instead of a stage inside the successor Job;
  - whether to propose different numbers.
- **Required change.** List this as an explicit owner choice with options: a held item to Slawomir; a separate review admission; or larger successor limits. Do not leave it to implementation.
- **Analysis bound.** The analysis profile uses one 4800 s window, equal for Job and stage and including queue. The approval packet should state that its expiry, cancellation or unknown cessation all map to the M385600 analysis-failure endpoint. That follows from the ruling but is not written.

**E3 (medium-low; prerequisite creep): owner identity in Stage 1.** The section 2 decision record requires an "authority-derived scope/grant". The organizational forest and grant resolution belong to 3-A. Stage 1 should state that it uses the delivered W16821 principal/scope seam and the existing deployment decision capability for Slawomir, recording what provenance exists. The full grant/mask provenance arrives with 3-A. Otherwise 1-B silently depends on Stage 3.

**E4 (low; wording): "existing agent-pool machinery".** Pool and model controls are Stage 4, and today only per-role declared workers exist. Section 2 and the ownership of 1-A should say the analysis role is bound through declared worker and profile configuration, as section 6 already says, without implying a Stage 4 dependency. The concrete participant and binding remains a deployment operand validated before execution, as M385599 requires.

**E5 (low; REC-8 fidelity).** REC-8 is narrow and faithful. Add the following before applying it:
- a decision link to the FINDING Stage 1 sections, as LIM-5 and REC-7 have;
- that new analysis and successor profiles are owner-selected before use (E1);
- that a candidate failing identity or digest validation is reported and is not recovery input;
- that verification output never becomes a new candidate;
- that the analysis role is not bound to a model or provider (M385599).

**E6 (low; implementation split).** 1-A then 1-B then 1-C is serial and each part is independently reviewable with fixtures. 1-B is the largest: the read/decision surface and decision record, plus the new admission and its profile binding. Splitting it into 1-B1 (decision record and surface) and 1-B2 (successor admission) is optional if review size becomes a problem. The manager, schema and composition files must have serial ownership, which the contract already requires. Stage 1 must not be selected before E1 approval, because 1-B "profile pinned" depends on it.
