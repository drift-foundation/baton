## E2 — expiry and review-only recovery: faithful to M385611

**Expiry after passed verification (section 3.7).** If the successor expires after verification passed but before review completes, including during a busy-reviewer wait, it:
- preserves the successful verification;
- holds an item for Slawomir;
- is treated as neither a verification failure nor an acceptance;
- starts no analysis and no automatic retry.

**Owner-authorized review-only admission.** A new explicit owner decision may authorize a linked review-only run. It binds:
- the original failure;
- the expired successor;
- the passed verification result and its digest;
- the exact candidate and base manifest;
- the approved verifier and context;
- the new decision.

Before admitting it, the manager revalidates evidence custody and integrity, and confirms the verification requirements are unchanged.

**Refusals and safety.** Missing, mismatched, invalidated or unknown evidence refuses and is reported, and never leads to blind re-verification. Expiry alone does not invalidate a passed verification. Implementation is never redispatched. Cancellation, ownership, prior review cessation and resource exclusion are rechecked, and an unknown state holds. A late old review result cannot satisfy the new run.

**Profile reuse is acceptable.** The review-only run reuses `stage1-verification-review/1` unchanged with the verification stage explicitly omitted.
- The definition holds only limits and policy, with no stage list. Omitting a completed stage therefore neither alters the profile nor creates a third one.
- Every bound is a maximum, and unused stage time is not transferred to review.
- The new admission gets new clocks while the old deadlines stay expired.
- A review-only expiry holds again with no automatic further admission.
- This meets M385612's requirement that the review-only path "name its reviewed finite execution contract" and not apply an unapproved profile.

One condition carries into implementation: the owner's review-only decision record must name the profile identity and digest plus the selected stage set, and admission must check that exact agreement. Section 3.7 and section 5's "owner decision names the profile; admission checks exact agreement" already require this. 1-B and 1-C acceptance must test it.

**Analysis endpoints.** Analysis failure, expiry or cancellation keeps the partial evidence and goes directly to Slawomir. Unknown cessation stays qualified and excludes any replacement. This is stated in section 3.2, section 5, 1-A acceptance and REC-8, matching M385600 and M385612.
