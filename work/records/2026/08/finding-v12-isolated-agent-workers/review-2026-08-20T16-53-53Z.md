invariant list now carries two more bullets: transport loss is never proof that

**3 — unqualified parallel child Work.** Resolved in place: "Parallel child Work

**5 — `file-discovery` only produced child Work.** Resolved by replacing the

offered, because it also removes the pressure to classify a discovery correctly

**6 — pre-claim and post-claim expiry conflated.** Resolved: the conformance

## One remaining residual

### Discovery drafts have no defined intake path when the source assignment is cancelled or its proposal rejected

**Decision-ready.** The superseded text carried an explicit non-loss guarantee:
"A separately filed discovery is not lost merely because its parent proposal is
rejected: its own ledger Work and materialization lifecycle remain independent
once `file-discovery` commits." That guarantee depended on the mechanism the
approver has now correctly removed — a draft becomes nothing until intake
accepts it — and the replacement does not restate it.

What the current model says: intake happens "at review/intake"; the
accepted-source path is spelled out ("For accepted source Work, the sealed
mounted output is reviewed with the source proposal…"); and "rejected or unused
drafts remain retained or quarantined according to policy". Cancellation is
handled elsewhere and never reaches review at all — "on cancellation or stale
generation it is preserved or quarantined according to policy and can never
publish by itself."

So an assignment that is force-stopped mid-flight, or whose proposal is
rejected on grounds unrelated to what the worker noticed, leaves its draft
findings quarantined with no stated path to a trusted reader. That is not a
competing rule; it is a dropped one. It matters here more than it would
elsewhere because this record's own motivating incident is exactly a
mid-assignment cancellation: W2938 was stopped because its contract was
superseded, and the observations made before the stop were worth keeping.

**Recommendation, one sentence either way.** Either state that sealed or
quarantined output from a cancelled assignment or rejected proposal is still
routed to trusted intake, with its disposition recorded as provenance — the
drafts are untrusted input to a trusted decision in every case, so reading them
creates no authority risk — or state explicitly that cancellation and rejection
forfeit drafts by policy, so nobody later assumes a guarantee the model does
not make.
