## Finding

### P1 — Operator launch surfaces still advertise the superseded inferred-role contract

The accepted W101 ruling now requires every launcher to name one explicit
role. The implementation correctly refuses a missing role, but two shipped
operator surfaces still say otherwise:

- `tools/acp-baton-bridge/README.md` says `baton.role` "may be omitted" when
  one instructed role exists;
- `tools/codex-event-bridge/src/main.mjs` renders the `--start-thread` usage
  operand as `[--role ROLE]`, which denotes optional input.

An operator following either surface receives a launch refusal from the very
boundary these instructions are meant to make reliable. Remove the obsolete
inference guidance, render `--role ROLE` as required, and make adjacent
missing/unheld-role wording agree with the explicit-only model. The two
additive operator-surface regressions currently fail on the stale surfaces.
