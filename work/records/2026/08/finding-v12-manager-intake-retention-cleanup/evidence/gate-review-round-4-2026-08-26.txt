W6629 -- evidence for review round 4's [P1].


THE DEFECT

  My per-surviving-row authentication loaded the committed `output.retain`
  result a row names, then compared artifact id, disposition and policy digest.
  Those three are LOCAL DECISION DATA rather than identities: two attempts can
  each hold an artifact called `artifact-1` and decide it `retain` under the
  same policy, entirely legitimately.

  So a row whose `retain_operation_id` was edited to name the OTHER attempt's
  authentic committed act matched on all three and was accepted. Cleanup for
  one attempt could draw its authorization from a decision made about another,
  and no journal row had to be forged anywhere -- the act it borrowed was real.


THE CORRECTION

  The committed act names the attempt it was made about, so that is the
  binding. `retentions_of` requires

      committed["attempt_id"] == attempt_id

  before it will return the decision, and reports a mismatch as integrity
  rather than as a value disagreement -- nobody's values disagree in this
  failure, which is what made it hard to see.

  The per-row membership model is unchanged, so a later policy replacing part
  of an earlier grouped decision still passes and a forged disposition or
  policy still fails.

THE SIBLING DOOR DOES NOT HAVE THIS HOLE

  Worth stating rather than leaving a reviewer to check.

  `intake_receipt_of` DERIVES its operation from the attempt --
  `intake_operation(attempt)` -- and compares the derived id against the stored
  one, refusing when they differ. The act is bound to the attempt by
  construction: there is no stored identity to edit into pointing elsewhere,
  because a receipt whose act does not derive from its own attempt is not that
  attempt's receipt.

  Retention rows are the other shape. They STORE the operation id, because one
  command decides a SET of artifacts and a later policy may replace part of it,
  so the identity cannot be re-derived from any single row. Storing an identity
  is what created the room for it to point somewhere else -- which is the
  difference this finding turns on, and the reason the same argument does not
  transfer to intake.
