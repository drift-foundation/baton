## Finding

### [P1] A retention row can borrow another attempt's committed act

`retentions_of` (`intake.py:891-911`) loads the committed `output.retain`
result named by each row, then compares artifact id, disposition and policy
digest. It does not compare the result's `attempt_id` with the attempt whose
rows were selected.

Those three compared values are local decision data, not globally unique
identities. If attempts A and B both use artifact id `artifact-1` and the same
policy/disposition, editing A's `retain_operation_id` to name B's authentic
committed act is accepted. Cleanup for A can therefore acquire authorization
from a decision made about B. No journal row needs to be forged.

Correction boundary: keep the per-surviving-row membership model, and also
require `committed["attempt_id"] == attempt_id` before returning the decision.
Report a mismatch as integrity. Preserve the passing partial-replacement and
forged-row cases.

Regression:
`IndependentContractReview.test_a_retention_cannot_borrow_another_attempts_committed_act`.
