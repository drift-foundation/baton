Restart proof is not connected to engine facts
==============================================

Repository search finds no production caller of CredentialHome.adopt,
read_state, resolved_delivery, materialize or discard_orphans outside
credentials.py. OciAdapter accepts an already-built Delivery, writes its own
lifecycle record after start, and tears it down after destroy.

CredentialHome.adopt compares a durable record with caller-supplied attempt and
runtime ids and with locally derived path constants. It never receives an
engine observation. OciAdapter.observe parses runtime identity and State only;
it does not read or return engine-reported mounts. Therefore no implemented
path can prove that the live container's credential mount sources/targets and
ephemeral root agree with the lifecycle record, nor can a disagreement drive
the required stop-worker plus bounded-orphan-cleanup path. The existing exact
adoption test proves agreement among self-authored documents and local files,
not agreement with the runtime.
