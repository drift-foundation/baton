## Findings

1. **P1 — freeze and collection have no immutable custody boundary.**
   `sealed_result` chmods and measures the original workspace output, then
   `collected_result` measures that same mutable path again. A host-side write
   after freeze changes the collected digest. Both locators also identify the
   live workspace rather than a staged copy. This contradicts the dossier's
   confirmed copy-before-observation boundary and breaks retry/restart
   identity.

2. **P1 — separate output names may overlap one tree.**
   `declared_outputs` rejects duplicate names but does not compare paths.
   Same-path and ancestor/descendant declarations are both accepted, contrary
   to the confirmed no-output-overlap boundary.

3. **Decision required — the credential-delivery boundary is unspecified.**
   W6630 supplies receiver/leak policy but no OCI injection mechanism. The
   adapter has no credential reference, ephemeral root or teardown owner.
   Choose and pin those facts before implementation. The dossier records a
   private ephemeral file plus fixed read-only bind mount as a proposal only.
