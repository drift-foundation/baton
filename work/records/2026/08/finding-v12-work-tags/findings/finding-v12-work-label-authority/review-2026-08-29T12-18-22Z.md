## [P1] Worker Manager projection inventory omits `creation`

`worker_manager.authority_port` validates projections against the exact union
of `PROJECTION_READ` and `PROJECTION_UNREAD`. The latter names W29400's
`labels`, but not the new `creation` member. A complete authority projection
therefore crosses the port with an unexpected member and is refused. Register
`creation` as unread metadata, exactly as this Work already did for `labels`;
that preserves the boundary without inventing manager semantics for it.

## [P1] The compatibility gate cannot find the canonical projection

`TheProjectionContractMatchesTheAuthorityItReads.authority_projection` parses
only `project_work` for a literal dictionary return. The earlier snapshot
correction made that method wrap `_projected`, where the dictionary now lives.
The extractor was not moved with it, so both compatibility tests fail before
they can compare or exercise the registry:

- `test_the_contract_names_exactly_what_the_authority_answers`;
- `test_a_whole_authority_projection_is_accepted`.

Both fail with `AssertionError: the authority's projection was not found`.
Point the extractor at the canonical `_projected` dictionary and retain exact
member equality; once corrected, it must also enforce the new `creation`
registry member above.
