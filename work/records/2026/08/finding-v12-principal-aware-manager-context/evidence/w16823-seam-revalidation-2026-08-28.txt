    first claim answer  : {"work_ref": {"authority_uuid": "0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef", "work_id": "0123abcd-W8"}, "participant": "org_a.worker", "generation": null}

    claim events        : [3, 5]
    Both events match the same answer, so a manager matching on the
    answer cannot say WHICH claim it just made. That is exactly the
    join W16821's own re-review refused as 'not an exact identity'.
