## Scope ruling — ACP admission belongs here

The added ACP absolute-path check is within this Work. The recorded confirmed
boundary explicitly applies the launcher contract to Codex-backed and
ACP-backed participants and forbids a context from inferring any contract
value from filesystem context. ACP `baton.binary` and `baton.config` become
that isolated agent's `BATON_BIN` and `BATON_CONFIG`; accepting relative values
would be the same defect at a third admission door.

The correction does not add a carrier, alter the shared instruction
projection, or change protocol behavior. It only applies the already ruled
absolute-source gate to values ACP already supplied. Splitting that known
instance into separate Work would leave one live violation of this dossier's
confirmed boundary for no independent scheduling reason.
