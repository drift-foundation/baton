The window is exactly as reported: `incidentFiled` is the DURABLE
acknowledgement and becomes true only after the awaited publication returns,
so two observers of one fence can both read it false and both publish.

Worth stating plainly: this window is the ROUND-2 correction's own
consequence. Routing the resume path through the shared settlement is what
made reconnect and a late `turn/completed` overlap naturally, and the
sequential late-notification case I added in that round runs only after the
first settlement has acknowledged — so it could never have witnessed this.


2. THE CORRECTION, IN TWO HALVES

  (a) JOIN, not a second publication. The in-flight promise is held on the
      orphan; a second observer awaits the SAME promise and returns its
      answer. One publication per fence, and both callers learn the same
      outcome.

  (b) RETRYABLE on failure. The handle is dropped unless the acknowledgement
      made it durable, so a runner that refused or threw is tried again by
      the next observer and by the restart re-file path. A shared promise
      without this half would have replaced double-filing with never-filing,
      which is worse and quieter.


3. FOUND WHILE CORRECTING, NOT IN THE REPORT

A runner whose `incident` REJECTED took the whole settlement path down with
it — out of `#settleTurn`, out of `#turnCompleted`, into an unhandled
rejection — so the incident was neither filed nor retried, and in the
notification path the rest of the handler never ran.

A throw is now a failed publication rather than a failed settlement: it is
logged, answered `false`, and retried. Nothing is lost, because `#fence`
makes the fence durable before anything asynchronous and `incidentFiled`
stays false.
