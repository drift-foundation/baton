Disposition: **signed off**. The first-review P1 is corrected. A rejected

## Confirmed correction

- `#turnCompleted` records `statusRefreshFailure` when its authoritative
  `readThread()` fails. Both `#publishReusableIdle` and `#drain` honor that
  fence, including the completion-before-`turn/start` deferred-publication
  ordering.
- `#scheduleStatusRefresh` captures the exact fence object. A newer status
  notification can clear or promote it while an older read is in flight, and
  the older result cannot recreate or overwrite the newer observation.
- An authoritative `idle` clears the transient fence, publishes reusable idle,
  and drains retained readiness. `systemError` or an unknown loaded status
  instead becomes the existing sticky terminal failure. `notLoaded` remains
  fenced and schedules another bounded reread.
- Reconnect uses the same status observer. It can recover the transient fence
  only from the resumed thread's authoritative status, while an already
  terminal configured context remains non-deliverable.
- Control status reports `status: unknown`, `ready: false`, and
  `deliverable: false` with the participant, session, failed turn, queue depth,
  and retry remedy. The operator documentation distinguishes this transient
  diagnosis from the full-managed-stack remedy for sticky `systemError`.
