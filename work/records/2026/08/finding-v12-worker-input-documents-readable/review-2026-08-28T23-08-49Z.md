## [P1] A lost allocation race raises raw `FileExistsError`

The attempt home and each child use a check-then-create sequence: test
`os.path.lexists`, then call `os.makedirs` without `exist_ok`. Two callers can
both observe absence. One creates the directory and the other receives
`FileExistsError` from the OS.

The deterministic additive case
`TheRuledTrustModel.test_first_allocation_race_answers_or_refuses_in_contract`
holds both callers after their shared absent-home observation. Current output
is one valid root answer and one leaked `FileExistsError(17, 'File exists')`.
This preserves isolation, so it is not another alias [P0], but it does not
satisfy ruling M34768's collision-refusal contract and can turn an ordinary
manager race into an unexpected fault.

Make create-or-collision one owned-directory operation at both home and child
levels. A caller losing the create race may validate and reopen the exact
directory, or receive `ContractRefusal`; it must not receive raw
`FileExistsError`. Add the equivalent child-entry race case and preserve the
now-valid no-link/exact-path checks, sequential restart reopen, distinct-attempt
concurrency, cleanup/retry, sibling isolation and readable/read-only delivery.
