Reviewer conclusion:
The original no-command-item symptom is real, but the first server-recording
diagnosis was wrong. The managed deployment used a different item family.
Live rawResponseItem/completed is a candidate evidence transport, not a proven
oracle: a bounded probe must first show both correlated items are emitted by
the deployed WebSocket server, and an operator must accept a strict,
fail-closed wrapper and matching contract before either gate can depend on it.
