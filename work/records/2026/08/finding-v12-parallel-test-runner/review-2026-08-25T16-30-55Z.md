## New finding

### R2 — High: an ordinarily exiting failed shard can still orphan descendants

**Observed.** `Run.drive()` calls `child.poll()` and, for every completed
leader, unconditionally deletes that leader's recorded group from
`self.groups` at `v12/python/tools/parallel_test.py:411`. This happens before
the parent harvests the shard report and computes whether the phase failed.
Final `shutdown()` therefore has no record of a failed shard's group when the
leader exited normally but a descendant remained alive.

That is not merely successful-test leak hygiene. A test can start a process,
fail an assertion, and let its unittest worker exit normally with return code
1. The runner reports the failure correctly but leaves the process behind.
This violates the explicit signal/**failure** descendant-reaping acceptance
boundary in `PLAN.md:29-31` and the no-worker-process failure-path boundary in
`FINDING.md:163-165`.

**Regression.** The additive
`AFailingRunAlsoLeavesNothingBehind.test_a_failed_shard_does_not_orphan_a_descendant_when_its_leader_exits`
at `v12/python/tests/tools/test_parallel_runner.py:523` drives the real runner
over a fake failing shard. The shard starts a TERM-ignoring descendant, waits
until that descendant publishes its PID, then fails normally. The regression
owns a final `SIGKILL` cleanup so exposing the defect does not leak a process.

Focused result:

```text

AssertionError: True is not false : the failed shard's descendant 15 survived the run
```

The full module has the same single failure; its other 32 tests pass:

```text
$ env PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m unittest tests.tools.test_parallel_runner
```

**Required correction.** A shard's failure verdict and process-group cleanup
must be resolved before the runner releases the protected ownership identity
for that group. Do not retain a bare PGID after reaping its leader and later
signal it: PID/PGID reuse could target an unrelated process. Choose a lifecycle
that preserves the ownership proof through cleanup, terminates descendants of
failed shards, then reaps/releases the leader. The new regression and full
runner-test module must pass before another review.
