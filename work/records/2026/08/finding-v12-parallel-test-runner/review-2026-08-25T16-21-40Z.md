## Finding

### R1 — High: interrupt cleanup can abandon a live shard descendant

**Observed.** `Run.shutdown()` sends `SIGTERM` to every live shard process
group, then removes a shard leader from `self.live` as soon as
`child.poll()` reports that the leader exited (`v12/python/tools/parallel_test.py:295`).
The later `SIGKILL` pass visits only the leaders still present in `self.live`.
Consequently, a descendant that ignores `SIGTERM` survives whenever its shard
leader accepts the signal and exits first. At that point the runner has also
discarded the only entry from which it tries to resolve the process group.

This violates the recorded acceptance boundary that signal/fault cleanup must
terminate every runner-owned child process group and leave no worker process
behind (`PLAN.md:19-20`, `FINDING.md:146-148`). The pre-existing interrupt test
covered only a cooperative descendant whose default `SIGTERM` action exits it,
so it could not expose this ordering.

**Regression.** The additive
`AnInterruptLeavesNothingBehind.test_it_kills_a_term_ignoring_descendant_after_its_shard_leader_exits`
in `v12/python/tests/tools/test_parallel_runner.py` starts a descendant that
installs `SIG_IGN` before publishing its PID, interrupts the real runner, and
checks the descendant after the runner exits. The test owns a final `SIGKILL`
cleanup so the intentionally exposed bug does not leak a process.

Focused result:

```text
$ env PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m unittest tests.tools.test_parallel_runner.AnInterruptLeavesNothingBehind.test_it_kills_a_term_ignoring_descendant_after_its_shard_leader_exits
AssertionError: True is not false : the TERM-ignoring descendant 15 survived the interrupt
Ran 1 test in 15.166s
FAILED (failures=1)
```

The complete runner-test module has the same single failure; its other 31
tests pass:

```text
$ env PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m unittest tests.tools.test_parallel_runner
Ran 32 tests in 18.303s
FAILED (failures=1)
```

**Required correction.** Retain runner-owned process-group identities
independently of whether their leaders remain alive. After the bounded TERM
grace period, send `SIGKILL` to every such group that may still exist, including
groups whose leaders have exited, then reap the leaders and remove the result
root. Keep the signal boundary restricted to process groups the runner itself
created. The new regression and the full runner-test module must pass before
another review.
