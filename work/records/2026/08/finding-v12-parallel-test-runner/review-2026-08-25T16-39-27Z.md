## New finding

### R3 — High: retirement deletes both cleanup handles before signaling the group

**Observed.** On ordinary completion, `Run.drive()` removes the child from
`self.live` before calling `retire()` (`v12/python/tools/parallel_test.py:462`).
`retire()` then removes the child's PGID from `self.groups` before calling
`signal_group()` (`v12/python/tools/parallel_test.py:402-404`). A `SIGINT` or
`SIGTERM` delivered in that interval invokes the installed handler, but
`shutdown()` sees neither the child nor its group. The handler exits 130 while
the completed shard's descendant survives.

The interval is small but it is exactly on the asynchronous-signal path whose
cleanup guarantee this Work owns. The state mutation must follow the cleanup
side effect, not precede it.

**Regression.** The additive
`AnInterruptLeavesNothingBehind.test_an_interrupt_during_retirement_cannot_cross_a_tracking_gap`
injects a real `SIGINT` immediately upon entry to the retirement
`signal_group()` call, after a normally completed fake shard has left a
TERM-ignoring descendant. The injection is armed only after the descendant
publishes its PID, so collection retirement is unaffected. Its final cleanup
force-kills the witness if the runner fails its obligation.

**Required correction.** Keep both the child and its protected group identity
registered until the cleanup signal has actually been issued. In particular,
do not remove the child from `self.live` before `retire()` and do not pop its
group before `signal_group()` returns. After the group has received `SIGKILL`,
the group entry may be released before reaping; the already-issued fatal signal
makes a later handler safe even though the leader remains in `self.live`.
Reap the leader and then release the live entry. Preserve the existing rule
that no bare PGID remains registered after its protecting leader is reaped.

The new injection regression and full runner-test module must pass before
another review.
