## Finding dispositions

- **R1 resolved:** abandoned-run TERM/grace/KILL retains process-group
  ownership through escalation.
- **R2 resolved:** completed shards kill descendants while the unreaped leader
  still protects the group identity.
- **R3 resolved:** retirement performs the cleanup side effect before releasing
  group/live tracking.
- **R4 resolved:** the asynchronous handler now records only the first pending
  signal. Spawn and registration complete before a scheduler checkpoint acts
  on it, so cleanup sees every process the runner created. A second checkpoint
  follows completed retirement batches, and the idle loop returns directly to
  the pre-poll checkpoint.

The handler remains installed through `run.shutdown()` and is restored only in
a nested `finally`. Thus another signal during bounded cleanup is recorded
rather than raising through TERM/grace/KILL or result-root removal. A signal
recorded after the last scheduler checkpoint converts an otherwise successful
result to `128 + signal` after cleanup.

Keeping an already-failed run's informative nonzero code rather than replacing
it with 130 is accepted: it cannot report false success, the pending signal
cannot bypass cleanup, and the existing failure evidence remains available.
