directly, before the attempt checks and before `_refused_start`. The statement
beside it that “nothing exists yet to settle” is true only when no other
provider has materialized anything. A canonical adapter may already hold a
`credential_delivery`; that root and its bearer registration exist before
`start` is called.

credential has no ending:


`evidence/w26291-missing-launch-credential-reproduction.py`.
