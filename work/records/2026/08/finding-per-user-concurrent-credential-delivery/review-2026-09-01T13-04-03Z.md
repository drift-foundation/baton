## [P1] The reader invents a narrower provider/reference contract than the manager

`tools/user_credentials.py` says its `_PROVIDER` set is the manager's identity
grammar, then restricts provider identities to 64 characters beginning with an
alphanumeric and containing only `[A-Za-z0-9._-]`. It separately limits opaque
references to 512 characters and rejects control characters.

The authoritative manager boundary does not say that. In
`worker_manager/boundaries.py`, `identity` is currently `text`: exact non-empty
encodable text, with no provider grammar or length ceiling. `resolved_delivery`
therefore accepts trusted pairs the new registry refuses. The independent
probe used the harmless provider `vault/team`: the manager returned
`({'slot': 'api', 'provider': 'vault/team', 'reference': 'opaque'},)`, while
`held_registry` refused that same pair because `/` was outside its invented
set. A valid trusted profile can consequently activate an assignment and then
fail only when its user-local source is materialized.

This is the second vocabulary the task was intended to avoid. Provider and
reference must stay exact and opaque under the manager's authoritative shape;
the registry reader may not add semantics or bounds merely because the common
examples fit them. Correct the owner or share the authoritative rule, and add
compatibility regressions driven from `credentials.resolved_delivery`,
including a provider containing `/` and an opaque reference wider than 512.

## [P1] A descriptor read failure escapes the promised typed, path-free refusal

`_proved_read` translates only `os.open` failures. Once the descriptor is
open, `os.fstat` and `_whole`/`os.read` execute inside a `try/finally` with no
`OSError` translation. An unreadable or failing regular file after open
therefore escapes as raw `OSError`, despite the approved boundary requiring a
safe typed source refusal.

The independent probe supplied a proved current-uid private regular descriptor
and made `os.read` raise `OSError("read failed")`. The public result was exactly
`OSError read failed`, not `SourceRefusal`. In the real arc this occurs after
assignment activation and while `CredentialHome.materialize` is creating the
delivery; the manager unwinds its root, but the source reader fails to provide
the typed cause its contract promises.

Translate `fstat` and read failures into bounded `SourceRefusal` prose that
names no host path, without hiding the existing type/owner/mode refusals. Add
injected `fstat` and `os.read` failure cases; chmod-only coverage exercises the
open failure and cannot reach this branch.

Relevant retained-candidate locations:

- `tools/user_credentials.py:370-403`
- `tests/tools/test_user_credentials.py:540-560`
