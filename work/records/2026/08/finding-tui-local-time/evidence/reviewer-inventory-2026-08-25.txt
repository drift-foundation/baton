INPUT-FORM HAZARD

  The projection has explicit UTC forms with Z, including fractional seconds,
  and Message timestamps in the canonical SQLite spelling with a space and no
  offset. Python datetime.fromisoformat returns the latter as naive. A bare
  naive_datetime.astimezone() assumes those fields are already local, which is
  false here. The formatter must attach timezone.utc to that canonical naive
  UTC spelling before converting.

RECOMMENDED DISPLAY SHAPES

  Compact: 18:36 MDT

  Use numeric %z when tzname() is empty. Compact Message/Event columns must
  measure the formatted values or disappear as whole responsive columns; a
  five-cell slice that removes the zone recreates the ambiguity.
