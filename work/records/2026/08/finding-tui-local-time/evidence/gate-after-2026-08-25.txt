      than 34 and `Time` drops at 34 rather than 30. Every rule W49 states is
      unchanged — fixed per-page allocations, one header, clipping inside a

      arithmetic now lives in `WIDEST` and `_time_cell`, so a later change to
      the cell's width moves one constant instead of six slices.

  tests/work/test_w93_runtime_state.py
      `test_the_teams_row_and_the_details_answer_different_questions` pinned
      the detail block's `Since` to the canonical `...T10:00:00Z` spelling.
      The property it states — the row is a duration, the detail is an
      instant — is unchanged and is now asserted against the local rendering,
      AND against the local rendering being absent from the compact row: a
      console that painted a local clock in an elapsed cell would be the same
      defect wearing different digits.
