descendants are still running. It then enumerates the process tree, signals the
owner, and waits for positive PID absence. A descendant may append a final
heartbeat during that interval. The later “stopped writing” check compares the
post-teardown files to the pre-signal counts and therefore calls that final
pre-teardown write evidence that the process survived.

Independent reproduction used a PATH-local `bwrap` stand-in that:

- runs the exact inner command and both descendants;
- recursively records and kills the complete descendant tree on `SIGTERM`;
- waits for its owned command and exits.

domain owner exited
