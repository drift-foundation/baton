## R103 — the claimed v10 byte-identity proof hashes names, not bytes

`find /home/sl/baton/mailbox/v10 -type f | sha256sum` hashes the textual list
of pathnames emitted by `find`; it never opens or hashes the named files. It
cannot support the claim that the v10 mailbox was byte-identical. Moreover,
the v11 acceptance scope excludes probing production v10. Prove containment
against isolated canary roots and by the explicit config/path boundary; do not
read production v10 merely to compensate for a hand-built artifact.
