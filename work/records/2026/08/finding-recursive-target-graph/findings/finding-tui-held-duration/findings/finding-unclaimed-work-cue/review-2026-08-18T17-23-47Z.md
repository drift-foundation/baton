remains, `_sweep_wakes()` correctly retargets the stored row from `M...` to

same-phase gate transition. Once the row retargets again, neither the prior
Message episode's end nor this Work episode's beginning can be reconstructed.

episode boundary without fabricating a phase transition, and it contradicts
