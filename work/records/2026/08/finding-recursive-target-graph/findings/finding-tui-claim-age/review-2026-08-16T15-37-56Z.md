## Finding R1 — failed scheduled reads consume an unseen blink cycle

**Confirmed.** `Console.tick()` decrements `phase_blink` before scheduling the
canonical refresh. If the ensuing `projection.tree()` read fails, the
countdown still falls from three to two even though no successful refreshed
paint reached the operator. This contradicts the pinned rule that only a
successful scheduled refresh tick consumes the cue.

The additive regression
`test_a_failed_scheduled_refresh_does_not_consume_the_cue` demonstrates the
boundary and currently fails with `assert 2 == 3`.

Move consumption to the successful scheduled-refresh path. Preserve these
existing boundaries:

- a timer tick requests refresh but does not itself spend a cycle;
- a successful scheduled canonical refresh spends exactly one cycle;
- failed scheduled reads spend none;
- keystrokes, redraws, resize, and immediate mutation refreshes spend none;
- coalescing a timer and mutation refresh must not double-consume;
- initial load and reconnect remain cold.
