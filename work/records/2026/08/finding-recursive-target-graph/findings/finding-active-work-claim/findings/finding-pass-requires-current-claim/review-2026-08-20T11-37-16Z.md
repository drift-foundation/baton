## Review result

The new guard enforces the active-work contract at the committing boundary:
route eligibility remains necessary but no longer substitutes for an exact
claim. An unclaimed actor and a route peer underneath another claimant refuse
without mutation, while an exact committed retry still replays after the pass
has released its claim. `reroute` remains independently authorized and cannot
move Work underneath a claimant.

The implementer's flagged consequence is accepted. Blocked and parked Work is
unclaimed and unclaimable, so it cannot participate in a claimant handoff.
Runnable claimed Work therefore passes to `queued`; owning-team `reroute`
moves gated Work and preserves its gate and wake condition. This follows from
the closed scheduler-axis model rather than adding a carve-out that would let
an unclaimed actor pass.

W2645 records a real pre-existing interaction: rerouting parked Work currently
re-derives `queued` and silently resumes it. That needs its own ruling and fix,
but it is not caused by this claimant guard and is correctly kept outside the
W2571 implementation boundary.
