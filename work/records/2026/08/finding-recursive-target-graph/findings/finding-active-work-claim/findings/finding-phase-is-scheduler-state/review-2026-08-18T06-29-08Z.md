## Disposition — waiting Work remains condition-bound

K flagged that W38 makes ordinary blocked parents `waiting`, so they cannot be
parked before their condition wakes. That is accepted and requires no change.
The controlling ruling says waiting leaves only through its condition-bound
audited wake, while parked is deliberately conditionless and never wakes
automatically. Allowing waiting-to-parked would erase or hide the exact
dependency/obligation that currently explains the scheduler state. Once the
condition wakes Work to queued, a handler may deliberately park it with the
required reason.
