# WS-2 pre-implementation decision challenge review

Reviewer: `baton.reviewer`
Date: 2026-08-14T21:27:46Z
Disposition: changes requested; implementation remains held

K revalidated the battery and reported no product contradiction, then named
ten intended mechanical mappings. Four require correction or a product ruling
before they can become implementation assumptions.

## R1 — write-dependent due notification does not meet the wake promise

K proposed deriving due state during reads while emitting the durable due
notification only from the first unrelated committing write at or after
`review_at`. If the mailbox is otherwise idle, that write may never occur, so
the responsible reviewer receives no event at the time the round becomes due.
The proposal therefore does not yet reconcile pure reads with the confirmed
actionable due notification and actual readiness mechanism.

Required response: give the smallest concrete alternatives and recommend one.
Do not implement a later-write pseudo-notification as though it met the ruled
behavior.

## R2 — separate assignments contradict exact `@` obligations

The finding currently says staged verification creates separate exact `@`
verification obligations. K proposed a separate assignment authority table and
state machine while leaving classic `@` obligations untouched. Those can be a
valid architecture only if an assignment is a specialized obligation sharing
the ruled actionable semantics, or if the existing decision is explicitly
superseded. Treating both rules as simultaneously live would make assignment
actionability, waiting, response, and projection semantics ambiguous.

Required response: compare reuse, specialization, and explicit replacement;
recommend the smallest coherent boundary.

## R3 — provider outcome applicability and closed-target edges are incomplete

K proposed requiring an outcome exactly when incoming dependency edges exist at
close time, while permitting an outcome on every close. This leaves two cases
undefined: whether a consumer may later add `blocked_by` to already-closed Work
that has no provider outcome, and what semantic meaning an outcome has on Work
that is not serving a dependent. Allowing meaningless outcome state or a
dependency whose terminal provider has no outcome makes projections ambiguous.

Required response: define whether new dependency edges may target closed Work,
and define exactly when provider outcome is required, optional, or refused.

## R4 — closed-Work reference restriction is worded too broadly

K said `follow_up_of` is the only operation that may reference closed Work.
Historical messages, links, audit, reads, and deliberate traversal must retain
their closed-Work references. The intended restriction appears to concern new
workflow mutations, not references generally.

Required response: state the exact mutation restriction and preserve all ruled
historical/navigation references.
