## [P1] An active filter overwrites the contextual Work tab row

On a re-rooted contextual page, `render` paints `[Jobs] [Messages] [Events]`
at row 1 and advances `table_top` at `src/baton_work/tui/app.py:2262-2268`.
The immediately following filter branch ignores that position, paints its
normalized clauses at row 1 again and resets `table_top` to 2 at lines
2269-2276. The resulting screen contains `filter: status=open [Events]`:
the filter text erases `[Jobs] [Messages]`, leaves a misleading fragment of
the third label, and removes the required local navigation disclosure from a
valid contextual Work page.

Budget the contextual tab row and normalized filter row independently and
monotonically before the table header. Both rows must remain visible, in a
stable order, when a filter survives re-rooting; neither may overpaint the
other. Preserve the existing narrow-terminal truncation and table viewport
budget beneath them.

The additive
`TestRootScopedTabs.test_a_filter_does_not_overwrite_the_contextual_tab_row`
in `tests/work/test_w6814_active_descendant_trail.py` reproduces the defect and
requires both complete disclosures.
