WHY THE ORIGINAL NO-PROJECTION-CHANGE PREMISE DOES NOT HOLD

  Combining runtime/teams + breadcrumb + detail/search in the TUI would:

    1. mix independent snapshots on one screen despite the existing tree
       consistency contract;
    2. grow reads with the number/depth of active Work;
    3. require the renderer to reproduce canonical filter semantics or display
       Work excluded by the selected filter;
    4. break the documented JSON/TUI statement that the tree is one canonical
       projection.

  Raw store access is explicitly forbidden. An additive active_trails field
  derived inside projection.tree is the bounded correction: public projection
  minor only, no protocol or SQLite schema meaning changes.
