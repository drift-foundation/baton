Remaining P1 reproduction
=========================

`resultManifest.properties` defines `completion_manifest_digest`, but its
`required` array ends at `manager_observed_at`. Control SPEC §8.4 calls the
member OPTIONAL. The shipped case
`TheReceiptBindsTheEnvelopeItValidated.test_a_receipt_may_still_omit_the_binding_today`
constructs a version 1.0 receipt with `disposition: completed`, omits the
member, calls `check_manifest_structure(..., "resultManifest")`, and passes.

That contradicts SPEC §7.3, which says the manager validates the worker
completion envelope and only then produces its receipt. `$defs.version` pins
major to 1 and minor to 0, so no versioned legacy/new distinction exists.

  - non-completed receipt: it may be absent if no worker envelope was
    published; if an envelope was validated, its digest is bound;
  - no version-vocabulary widening for this migration;
  - update the 96 measured fixtures and add/retain a negative completed-without-
    digest regression.
