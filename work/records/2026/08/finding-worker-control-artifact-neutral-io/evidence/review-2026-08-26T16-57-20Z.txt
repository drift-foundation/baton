P1: output.json has no possible publisher under the revised contract
====================================================================

The owning umbrella ruling at FINDING.md:1753-1755 says the WORKER writes every
durable result below /output/ and publishes output.json last.

The revised control SPEC §8.4 identifies that file with
baton.worker-manifest/result. The resultManifest schema requires, among other
members:

  freeze_operation
  manager_observed_at
  artifact references carrying manager-custody locator/digest/length

Those facts are created by the manager's output.freeze after the worker is
quiescent. The worker cannot publish them before quiescence. The current
manager sealing path demonstrates the ownership: sealing.sealed_result
composes freeze_operation and manager_observed_at and publishes sealed.json in
manager custody.

The conformance revision chooses the opposite publisher from the pinned
ruling: A-output-json-published-last stimulates control operation output.freeze,
and A-interrupted-publish-leaves-no-output-json explicitly stops the manager
during publication. PROGRESS then tells W6633 that the worker must publish the
same file while also populating manager-owned artifact references. These three
accounts cannot all be true.
