## Source path

- `tools/acp-baton-bridge/src/config.mjs`: validates and retains the four
  `baton` values; accepts `agent.env` as an independent arbitrary string map.
- `tools/acp-baton-bridge/src/acp_agent_session.mjs`: spawns with
  `{...process.env, ...agent.env}`.
- `tools/acp-baton-bridge/src/acp_baton_bridge.mjs`: passes only
  `role.instructions` into the prompt renderer.
- `tools/acp-baton-bridge/src/baton_readiness.mjs`: renders the action locator,
  role prose, and standing-policy cue; no launcher values.
- `conf/acp-claude.template.json` and `conf/acp-gemini.template.json`: omit the
  four environment values.
- The live deployment's `pc.code` template manually duplicates the four
  values, while `baton.claude` does not.  That difference is deployment drift,
  not a product guarantee.
