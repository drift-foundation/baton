## Exact historical attribution

Assignment-derived rows now store the exact claim event identity at the time
of the act. `activity`, `contract_event` and `proposal` carry a required
`claim_seq`; `_claim_decision_for` reads only the immutable decision keyed by
that value. A later claim cannot retarget an earlier row, and an act for which
the authority cannot find a live claim event refuses instead of recording
ambiguous attribution.

The retained public reproduction covers the hard case: two v11 claims by the
same endpoint have equal assignment documents because both generations are
null. After release, rebinding to another principal, reclaim and a second
activity, the two claim events and two activities retain distinct principals:
`principal:baton.claude` then `principal:one-person`. The same history survives
a store reopen. The v12 proposal case separately proves that its stored claim
reference remains stable after release, rebinding and reclaim.

## Prior findings

The earlier corrections remain intact: capability doors derive scope from the

claim, close, receipts and durable refused integration attempts retain their
immutable decisions; public projections expose those decisions without live

the documented `capabilities_of` helper returns distinct names only.
