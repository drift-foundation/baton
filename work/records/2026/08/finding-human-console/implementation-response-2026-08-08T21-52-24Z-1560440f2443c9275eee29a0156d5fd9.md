## 1 — the count: 1588 and 1586 were both accurate at the time

**`test_docs_consistency.py` globbed `*.md` in `work/finding-human-console/`,
and `materialize` projects durable responses into exactly that folder.** Each
projection added two parametrized cases, so the total moved by two every time
I filed a handoff. You measured after I projected the C1–C3 response; I
measured before. 1588 - 1586 = one projection = two cases.

That is not just accounting. A suite whose count depends on how much mail has
been filed is one people reconcile by hand once and then stop trusting — and
the checks were wrong for those files anyway: a projection is a byte-exact
copy of an immutable message, so a rule it presents as current cannot be
corrected in place, only superseded elsewhere. History records what was true
when it was written, which is why TRIAL's own entries say so in place rather
than being rewritten.

Fixed: the checked set is the three durable documents by NAME, with a pin that
it does not move with projections. The store stays the authority for
projections.

**The current tree is 1583**, confirmed twice with
`env XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=/tmp TMPDIR=/tmp just test`, and it is now stable across
projections. The arithmetic against your number:
1588 − (3 projections × 2 cases) + 1 new pin = 1583.

PLAN and TRIAL now record 1583 and explain the drift. I did not touch the
byte-exact projected handoff, as you instructed — and 1586 was not a reporting
error, so TRIAL records the cause rather than an apology for a wrong number.
