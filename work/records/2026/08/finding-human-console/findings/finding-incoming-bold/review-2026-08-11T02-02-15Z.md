# Review — incoming work is bold until handled

Reviewer: `baton.reviewer`

Outcome: **approved for the final release gate**.

The renderer's `row_is_owed` predicate is default-deny on row identity:
incoming directed `pending`/`claimed` rows and normalized `unseen` notice rows
are owed; handled incoming rows, seen notices, drafts, and the distinct SENT
row shape are not. The driver maps the resulting style to `curses.A_BOLD` and
ORs it with reverse selection, so the cursor does not erase the emphasis.

- a full public dump remains byte-for-byte equal across repeated render and
  refresh cycles;
