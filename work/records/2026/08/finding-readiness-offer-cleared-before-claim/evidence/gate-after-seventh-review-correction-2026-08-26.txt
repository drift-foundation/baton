W11910 -- evidence for the seventh review's [P1], and one thing left uncovered.


THE DEFECT

  The sixth correction let a live non-Work action pass a deferred Work at the
  queue head, and `#drain` starts THAT candidate's turn. If its `turn/start`
  loses its response the catch marks that candidate ambiguous -- correctly --
  but every reconciliation path then looked at `queue[0]`, which is the
  retained Work, and `#pastTheClaimSlot` skips ambiguous candidates on every
  later retry.

  So a turn the server actually created was never bound to this dispatcher:
  completion could not be correlated through `activeTurn`, the delivery could
  not settle through its normal path, and the lane sat behind an active server
  status and a retained event.


THE CORRECTION: AMBIGUITY FOLLOWS THE CANDIDATE, NOT THE QUEUE POSITION

  Two sites assumed the ambiguous delivery was the head.

    #reconcileTarget   `state.queue[0]?.ambiguous` -> the first ambiguous
                       entry, wherever it sits
    #drain             the head -> the first ambiguous entry if there is one,
                       reconciled BEFORE any replay; if it turns out not to
                       have been delivered it becomes an ordinary queued event
                       again and the head decides as usual

  B keeps the head and its original in-flight identity throughout.

THE ADDITIVE CASE

  The review's own regression is kept exactly as written. Beside it, the half
  the review REQUIRED and nothing asserted -- "preserve B at the head with its
  original in-flight identity":

    the deferred Work keeps the head and its in-flight identity throughout a
    passing action's ambiguity

  It re-enqueues B's unchanged offer afterwards and requires `in-flight`. If B
  had lost that identity, the producer's next level-triggered re-send would be
  accepted as a NEW delivery and B would queue behind itself -- the duplicate
  the retention exists to stop, reached by a path the sixth correction opened.


AND ONE THING IS LEFT UNCOVERED, DELIBERATELY NAMED

  The drain-selection half covers a real path the reconcile half does not.
  `turn/start` failing while the client is still CONNECTED reconciles
  immediately in the catch; if the transport dropped with the response, that
  branch is skipped by its own `client.connected` guard and the next ordinary
  drain is the only thing that runs. With the head-only selection that drain
  looked at B and skipped the ambiguous candidate behind it on every retry.

  I WROTE A CASE FOR IT AND IT HUNG. Constructing the disconnect mid-`turn/
  start` left the suite unable to terminate -- a pending reconnect or an
  un-stoppable bridge -- and a hanging test is worse than an uncovered path, so
  it was removed rather than shipped.

  So the drain change is reasoned, not measured, and this says so rather than
  letting the green suite imply otherwise. Two ways to settle it, and the
  choice is the reviewer's: cover that path with a construction that
  terminates, or remove the drain selection as unreachable-in-practice and
  accept that a disconnect-ambiguous non-head waits for the next reconnect.
