THE CORRECTION

  tools/codex-event-bridge/src/codex_baton_bridge.mjs

  The guard was

      if (action.claimed !== undefined && typeof action.claimed !== "boolean")

  and is now two guards rather than one, because these are two different
  authority faults and the older one already had a name:

      if (action.claimed === undefined)          -> "carries no claimed verdict"
      if (typeof action.claimed !== "boolean")   -> "claimed is not a boolean"

  A WRONG type is an answer this build cannot read; an ABSENT field is an
  answer the authority did not give. Both take the bounded `uncertain` path
  that the third and fourth reviews established, so nothing new was invented
  for this: an envelope that cannot be typed proves neither that the episode
  still exists nor that the claim slot is free.

  ONE EDIT, BOTH FAMILIES. The ACP bridge imports and re-exports this exact
  validator (`baton_readiness.mjs:13-21`) and validates through `waitOnce`
  before `episodeVerdict` reads `entry.claimed === true` at
  `baton_readiness.mjs:133,137`. The finding's "one implementation, imported by
  both families" decision is why this is one guard and not two.
