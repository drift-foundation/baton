## [P1] A deferred Work at the queue head swallows a later obligation

`EventBridge.#drain` selects only `state.queue[0]` at
`tools/codex-event-bridge/src/event_bridge.mjs:549`. When the current authority
reports that entry as unclaimed Work B beside claimed Work A, the `deferred`
branch at lines 585–589 retains B, schedules another read of the same head and
returns. If an obligation is admitted behind B, no drain reaches it until A's
claim disappears.

That is the Work-only gate governing the whole FIFO by queue position. It
contradicts the confirmed boundary that obligations, due trials and pokes keep
their existing delivery semantics beside a Work claim. The existing case `a
non-Work obligation is not held behind the participant's claim` proves only an
obligation that starts at the queue head; it never crosses the retained Work
state W11910 introduced.

The impact is an indefinite managed-lane stall, not just ordering preference:
A can remain the live claimed Work while B waits for A to end, and a directed
obligation the same participant must answer to finish A waits behind B. ACP's
loop already continues after a `deferred` Work and therefore does not reproduce
this gap.

Required correction: retain B and its exact in-flight identity, but allow a
later v11 non-Work readiness action whose own current canonical entry is live
to pass the Work-only claim-slot barrier. Do not rotate another unclaimed Work
or a generic non-readiness event past B. Preserve claimed-Work recovery
promotion and the existing queued/starting/ambiguous/active, W99 quarantine and
orphan-fence retention rules.

The additive regression `a deferred Work at the queue head does not hold a
later obligation` is at
`tools/codex-event-bridge/test/claim_slot.test.mjs:282`. It first proves B is
retained behind claimed A, then queues an obligation and requires exactly that
obligation to become a turn while B remains at queue depth one.
