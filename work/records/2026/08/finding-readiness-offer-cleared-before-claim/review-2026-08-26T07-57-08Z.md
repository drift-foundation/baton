## [P1] Ambiguous delivery behind the retained head is never reconciled

When `#pastTheClaimSlot` returns a live action behind deferred Work B,
`#drain` reassigns its local `queued` variable to that passing candidate at
`tools/codex-event-bridge/src/event_bridge.mjs:599-607` and starts its turn.
If `turn/start` fails non-protocol-ambiguously, the catch correctly marks that
candidate ambiguous at line 655.

The reconciliation paths still assume ambiguity belongs to `queue[0]`.
`#reconcileTarget` examines only the head, which is the non-ambiguous retained
B, and `#pastTheClaimSlot` explicitly skips the ambiguous candidate at line
883 on every later retry. Thus a turn that the server actually created is
never bound, while its readiness event remains queued and in flight.

This is not only stale queue state. The bridge has lost ownership of a live
managed turn: completion cannot be correlated through `activeTurn`, the
delivery cannot settle through its normal path, and the participant's lane can
remain stuck behind the active server status and retained event.

Required correction: ambiguity must follow the exact candidate whose
`turn/start` was attempted, even when that candidate is not the queue head.
Reconcile it by client message id before any replay, dequeue and bind it if
delivered, and preserve B at the head with its original in-flight identity.
Preserve the sixth correction's positive and negative pass cases and all W99 /
W4303 terminal-settlement behavior.
