## Finding

### [P1] Same authority is not proof that the next action is the exact recovery wake

`AcpSettlement.admits()` compares only the current envelope's
`authority_uuid` with the fence's recorded authority. `runBridge` calls that
gate once for the whole envelope; when it matches and the existing fence is a
durable verified `claimed` fence, `fenced()` is false and every fresh action
may proceed to per-action revalidation and a prompt.

That widens the approved W11910 exception. The ruling permits redelivery only
for the unspent recovery wake for the same participant and assignment episode.
It does not authorize spending a successor claim or a later neighboring wake
solely because the same ledger answered. The finding's still-live acceptance
also says later wakes remain retained until the exact claim is reconciled.

Two end-to-end probes reproduce the consequences:

- `/tmp/w55705-same-authority-successor-probe.mjs` first strands
  W1/episode 11, then presents claimed W2/episode 22 from the same authority.
  Both W1 and W2 reach `promptText` before the old fence is reconciled. Only
  post-turn settlement replaces the W1 fence with W2 and files its incident.
- `/tmp/w55705-later-wake-probe.mjs` presents the exact claimed recovery wake
  followed by a poke in one envelope. The failed recovery wake correctly
  remains eligible, but the poke also reaches `promptText`. Settling that poke
  changes the same W1/episode-11 fence from `claimed` to `held` and files a
  second incident for the same surviving Work and episode.

The pre-delivery gate must preserve the distinction between authority identity
and fence/action identity. While a marker exists, reconcile the recorded claim
before admitting any non-exact action. Only the exact unspent recovery wake may
take the approved redelivery path; a same-authority successor or neighboring
action remains retained, and a successor is recorded through reconciliation
before any turn is spent. Add end-to-end cases for both probes, including the
one-incident assertion for the original fence.
