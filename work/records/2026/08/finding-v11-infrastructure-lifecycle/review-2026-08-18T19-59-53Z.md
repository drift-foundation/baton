## P1 — the non-session-leader guard regression is environment-dependent and vacuous

`test_a_service_that_is_not_its_own_session_leader_is_not_broadened` assumes
the pytest process is not its own session and process-group leader. That is not
a portable test precondition: in the current focused runner both `sid` and
`pgid` equal `os.getpid()`, so the test fails before reaching any behavioral
assertion.

Even when the environmental assumption happens to hold, the test only reads
`_proc(os.getpid())` and asserts the observed ids. It never calls
`_terminate`, never observes whether `killpg` or `pidfd_send_signal` was used,
and therefore cannot detect removal or inversion of the guard its name and
docstring claim to prove.

Replace this with a deterministic behavioral test whose process topology is
created by the fixture or whose signal primitives are safely observed. It
must prove an owned matching process that is not both its own session and
group leader takes the single-process pidfd signal path and never the group
signal path. Do not use the pytest process's incidental session topology as a
precondition, and do not risk signalling the suite's real process group.
