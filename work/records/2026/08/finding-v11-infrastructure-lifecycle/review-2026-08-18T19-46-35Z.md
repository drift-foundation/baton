## P1 — stop leaves managed service descendants alive

The controller launches every configured service with `start_new_session=True`,
but `_terminate` sends `SIGTERM` only to the recorded group leader through its
pidfd. A managed service may spawn a long-lived child; the deployed ACP bridge
does exactly that for its agent process. When the leader exits normally, stop
removes the service from lifecycle state and reports success even though the
child remains alive in the controller-created session.

Lifecycle ownership must cover the process group/session that the controller
itself created. Termination must retain the existing fail-closed leader
identity check, signal only that owned group, wait boundedly for the managed
group to drain, and keep truthful state when any member remains. It must not
broaden into process discovery or signal a reused/unowned group.

The additive regression
`test_stop_terminates_a_service_process_group` starts one managed service that
spawns a child, proves the child is alive, invokes stop, and requires both to
be gone. Current stop returns success while the child remains alive for the
full three-second assertion window.
