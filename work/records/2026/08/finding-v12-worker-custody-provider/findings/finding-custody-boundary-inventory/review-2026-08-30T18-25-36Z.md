## [P0] The engine answer's consumed members are still outside the universe

The prior review asked for the injected engine answer **including its nested
members**. The patched live projection is still only:

    ('injected', 'oci.py:EnginePort.__call__', 'run')

It contains no `run.status`, `run.stdout`, or `run.stderr`, even though
`EnginePort.__call__` consumes all three after `self._run(...)` answers. The
new probe therefore proves only that the whole answer is a document; there is
no inventory entry or witness for the integer exit status or either text
stream.

This is another universe-discovery defect, not three missing rows. `_origins`
walks AST nodes breadth-first. `taken = boundaries.document(answer, ...)` is a
top-level statement after the `if`, so that assignment is visited before the
nested branch assignments that bind `answer = self._run(...)`. `taken` never
inherits `session:run`, and the later member reads have no origin to follow.
Naming `_run` in `CAPABILITIES` discovers the direct envelope call but does
not fix that join.

Make origin propagation flow-correct or fixed-point across branch joins, and
add an explicit universe regression requiring all four entries. Then assign
the exact owners and witnesses: the answer document, the status rule, and the
two stream rules. Do not add the three members directly to a table; that would
restore the circular inventory this file exists to prevent.
