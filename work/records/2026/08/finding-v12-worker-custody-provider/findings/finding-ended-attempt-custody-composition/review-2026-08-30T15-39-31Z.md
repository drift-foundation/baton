## [P0] Keep the root identity held across destructive use

`_proved_own(place, ...)` validates a pathname, returns it, and `_remove(place)`
reopens the pathname afterwards. A replacement in that interval turns the
proved root into a sibling alias, and `_remove` traverses it.

The additive
`test_execution_cleanup_does_not_follow_a_root_replaced_after_proof` performs
that replacement at the exact proof/use seam and delegates to the real remover.
The sibling sentinel is deleted before the walk ends in a raw filesystem
error. Static `islink`/`realpath` checks cannot close this interval.

descriptor-relative traversal/removal rooted at proved directory descriptors,
with child identity checks at use — rather than reopening mutable names after
proof. A broad error catch after traversal cannot restore deleted material.
