## [P0] An authentic allocation answer can be retargeted

`AllocatedRoots` in `worker_manager/workspaces.py` is a mutable `dict`
subclass. `attempt_custody_root` in `worker_manager/custody.py` checks its exact
type and then trusts the paths currently stored in it. A caller can therefore:

1. obtain a real `AllocatedRoots` from `assignment_workspace`;
2. create an unrelated manager-owned parent with `inputs` and `workspace`
   children;
3. replace the authentic answer's two members with those paths; and
4. obtain a valid `CustodyRoot` for the unrelated workspace.

This passes the nominal-type check and all retained shape/ownership checks.
The authority-bearing answer must be immutable, or custody must derive the
exact paths from manager-owned durable attempt state rather than from mutable
caller-held members.
