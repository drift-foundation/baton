IMPLEMENTATION CONSTRAINT FOUND

`projection.links` does not own a read snapshot. A depth-N TUI crawl made from
N cached calls can mix commits and has no bound under fan-out. The recommended
patch adds a dedicated bounded `dependency_neighborhood` projection under
`_read_snapshot` and leaves the existing public `links work=` shape compatible.
