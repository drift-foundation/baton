=== 3. WHAT THE TWO FAILURES ARE, diagnosed WITHOUT exposing text
  A boolean sieve: which of a fixed set of phrases appeared, never the
  phrases themselves and never the surrounding output.

  CLAUDE -> credential-refresh-blocked.
    The sieve matched OAuth, expired and refresh, and nothing else.
    The runtime authenticates, finds its OAuth token expired, tries to
    REFRESH it, and cannot: the credential is mounted read-only.

    THAT IS A FINDING ABOUT THE DELIVERY SHAPE, not about the
    credential, which is why it has its own category rather than being
    filed under 'authentication'. A read-only credential mount works
    only for a STATIC credential. An OAuth runtime rotates its own, and
    a copy taken at one moment is a snapshot of a moving thing.

  CODEX -> network.
    'Connection failed: error sending request' against its backend,
    while preflight proves from inside a container that both provider
    endpoints answer. Not diagnosed further: the raw probe outlived a
    ten-minute bound, which is the same non-termination the harness
    already bounds and the reason it does.

=== 4. WHAT THIS TELLS W6634, and it is the most useful thing here
  W6634's approved credential delivery materializes assignment-private
  files and mounts them READ-ONLY at a fixed root. On this evidence that
  shape is sufficient for neither real runtime:
    Codex will not START with its state directory read-only;
    Claude starts, authenticates, and cannot REFRESH.
  Both want to write beside their credential. A delivery that forbids it
  supports static secrets only — which may be the right answer, but it
  is a decision the design has not yet made explicitly.
