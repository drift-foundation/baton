## P2 — a pane key leaves an earlier `Ctrl-W` prefix armed

The new Tab branch runs before `ctrl_w_pending` is consumed. After the input
sequence `Ctrl-W`, `Tab`, the pane cycles but `ctrl_w_pending` remains true.
The next ordinary `j`, `k`, or arrow is therefore interpreted as the second key
of the stale Ctrl-W chord instead of acting in the pane Tab selected.

Tab and Ctrl-W are alternatives; completing one gesture must leave the other
gesture idle. Consume/cancel the pending prefix when handling either Tab form,
then perform the ruled pane cycle. Cover both Tab and Shift-Tab, and ensure the
following selection key uses the newly focused pane's ordinary behavior.
