`_publish` (1120), but `custody` (1843) does not include that path, and install's
failure cleanup (2206 onward) only removes owned justfile/runtime. Independent
probe over ConnectedCreationCase reproduces all three consequences:

1. Pre-existing regular install-inputs.json containing FOREIGN is overwritten;
   installation succeeds and publishes the selector.
2. Pre-existing install-inputs.json.tmp symlink to a foreign file is followed by
   temporary.write_text; foreign bytes are overwritten. os.replace then moves
   that symlink to install-inputs.json and installation still succeeds.
3. Injected instance.create refusal after input publication leaves
   install-inputs.json behind despite no selector. This contradicts the new
   comment claiming same unwind and no leftover input on failed install.
