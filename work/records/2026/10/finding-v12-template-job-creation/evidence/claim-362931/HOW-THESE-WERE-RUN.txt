R3 is reproduced here, not fixed
--------------------------------

`TheRequestedTaskDoesNotReachTheWorker` in tests/tools/test_creation_ports.py
asserts the mechanism from the owning code, in three facts and one
consequence. It is EVIDENCE FOR a specification gap, not a fix:

  * `single_worker._task_bytes(given["task_document"],
    manifest["human_contract"])` reads the task from the DEPLOYMENT's
    configured path at static preflight and holds it as immutable bytes. Its
    signature takes a path and a manifest artifact and no Job operand.
  * `stage_execution._Stage.prepare_context` delivers exactly
    `worker.given["task_bytes"]`.
  * `contracts.manifest` classifies `human_contract` as JOB INTENT -- "which
    task, by content digest" -- so it is INSIDE `job_input_identity`, which
    is what `_disagreements` compares against the Job's `input_digest`.
    `JOB_INPUT_EXCLUDED` does not contain it, and a case asserts that.
  * consequence, driven through `stage_execution.servable_workers`: a Job
    whose input identity named the REQUESTED task is refused for EVERY stage
    with "the submitted input". So the gap cannot be closed by deriving a
    different digest.

PROGRESS.md states what that implies for scope and what it would take.
