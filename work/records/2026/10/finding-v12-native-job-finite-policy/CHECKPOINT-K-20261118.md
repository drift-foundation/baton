The origin is the EARLIEST such instant over all of the stage's episodes, so replacement and correction episodes never reset it. Why not the scheduler reservation: it exists only on the pooled path and precedes the authorization; the owner's offer instant is the authorization itself and is uniform across every composition. Window: between the owner's commit and this store's receipt no origin is readable here; nothing can advance past `offered` until that receipt exists (`_owed` requires it), so no later boundary ever runs without the origin, and the next tick adopts it with the owner's instant intact.
2. **Trusted clock model and persisted elapsed evidence.** The Job store persists a monotonic WATERMARK (`meta` key `control.watermark`, an additive key; `_validate` reads only kind and version): the latest instant any control evaluation of this store observed. It is advanced under the store's own lock by the sweep's control pass and by every write-side guard that evaluates a deadline. Known elapsed = max(now, watermark) − origin. `now < watermark` (rollback, or a restart on an earlier clock basis) is `uncertain`: new admission is held and reported; elapsed and reached deadlines are decided from the watermark and are never less than previously known; existing cancellation/expiry fences and their stop reconciliation continue untouched. An instant this build cannot parse is `uncertain` too. The reviewer's example (origin+200 observed, then origin+100) keeps elapsed 200 and holds. A manager outage is reported as a gap between `watermark`/`observed_at` and the deadline; it is never described as continuous enforcement.
