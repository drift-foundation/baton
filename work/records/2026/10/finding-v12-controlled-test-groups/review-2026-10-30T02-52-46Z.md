## R1 — message-bearing subtest failures are still counted as passes (P2)

Location: `v12/python/tools/parallel_test.py`, `base_id` and `run_shard` outcome serialization.

`base_id` removes only the ` (parameters...)` suffix. Standard unittest `with self.subTest("named diagnostic")` emits `parent.test [named diagnostic]`, so the failure is recorded under an identity outside the collected set while the collected parent remains in `passed`. Independent reproduction: one collected and executed test, one passed parent, one failed synthetic identity. The overall verdict remains FAILED, but exact per-test accounting and the advertised one-outcome-per-collected-test claim are false.

Required: bind subtest outcomes to their actual parent identity at the result/serialization boundary, including message-only, parameters-only, and message-plus-parameters subtests; preserve diagnostics separately. Verify failures, errors and skips cannot leave their parent reported passed. Exercise the real runner on a disposable suite and ensure no synthetic uncollected failure IDs or double-counted passed parents. Do not infer identity by truncating arbitrary diagnostic text.
