# The shutdown declaration is ADMISSION-bounded, not execution-bounded

Claim 256097, baton.claude. Review 2026-09-24T10:42:09Z [R1]: "any positive
remainder admits the complete fence/inspect/remove/custody/discharge sequence
with no remaining-time propagation… If the current interfaces cannot carry a
needed bound, document that precise interface gap and a bounded correction
proposal rather than claiming the total is enforced."

**They cannot, and this is that document.** My PLAN said "the declaration is
inside the selected total". That was stronger than the evidence and is
withdrawn: what is bounded is ADMISSION — whether a new attempt's ending is
STARTED — and not the execution of one already admitted.

## The exact interface gap

Every layer between the supervisor and the engine takes the same three
operands, and none of them takes a deadline:

| layer | signature |
| --- | --- |
| router | `stage_execution.abandon_attempt(*, attempt_id, reason, stage)` |
| composition | `single_worker._Operations.abandon_attempt(*, attempt_id, reason, stage)` |
| worker | `single_worker._SingleWorker.abandon_attempt(*, attempt_id, reason, stage)` |
| core | `intake.abandon_attempt(store, port, adapter, *, attempt_id, reason, retention_policy_digest)` |

So a caller holding seconds has nowhere to put them. The per-operation bounds
that DO exist are the package's own constants, not caller operands:
`custody.CUSTODY_SECONDS = 1800`, `CUSTODY_ACT_SECONDS = 2100`,
`CUSTODY_RECLAIM_SECONDS = 120`, `CUSTODY_STOP_SECONDS = 5`. The engine port
takes `seconds` per vector, and the adapter chooses it.

**The worst case is therefore bounded by those constants and not by the run's
total.** One admitted declaration can exceed a small remainder — a custody act
alone is allowed 2100 seconds — and the supervisor cannot currently prevent it
from starting once any positive remainder exists.

## Why the supervisor cannot close this on its own

Three things it could do, and each is wrong:

  * **Refuse unless the remainder exceeds the worst case.** That reads
    another package's private constants and would refuse nearly every
    shutdown, leaving runtimes stranded to protect a clock.
  * **Interrupt mid-sequence.** The sequence commits durable records between
    external calls precisely so an interruption is resumable; tearing it down
    from outside would be the manager stopping its own ending halfway rather
    than the product's resumable semantics.
  * **Run it in a thread with a timeout.** The call would continue after the
    supervisor stopped waiting, so the bound would be on the REPORT and not on
    the work — which is the false claim this page exists to withdraw.

## The bounded correction proposal

An optional `seconds` operand threaded through the four signatures above to
the adapter's engine calls, where a per-vector bound already exists:

  1. `intake.abandon_attempt(..., seconds=None)` — passed to the adapter's
     `destroy_abandoned` and to `_normalized`'s custody acts, each clamped to
     the package constant so a caller can only ask for LESS;
  2. the three compositions forward it unchanged;
  3. the supervisor supplies `total - elapsed` and, when that is smaller than
     a floor the core states, records the attempt unresolved without starting
     it — which is the admission rule already in place, now with a floor that
     means something.

`None` preserves today's behaviour exactly, so no accepted caller changes.

**Not proposed:** clamping by reading another package's constants from the
supervisor, cancelling a committed sequence from outside, or reporting a bound
the work does not actually obey.

## What this claim does instead

  * the admission rule stays and is proved: with no remainder nothing starts,
    effect counts unchanged; with a remainder the same attempt settles;
  * the PLAN's overclaim is withdrawn here and in `PLAN.md`;
  * the proposal above is offered for disposition. `intake.py` is owned by
    baton.claude under owner 255814; the signature change also touches the
    two composition files, which are owned. **No owner gate is assumed** —
    this is a semantic change to an accepted public operation, so it is
    proposed rather than taken.

## Still owed regardless of the ruling

The supervisor-level stranded and expired cases, and the small-positive-
remainder case with a delayed fake boundary, which is what would MEASURE the
executed bound rather than the admission. They are listed in `PLAN.md`.
