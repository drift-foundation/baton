## P1: baseline and proposed completion predicate contradict existing lifecycle

false. A new expiry transition may still be needed, but reuse these states and
distinguish revocation causes/lifecycle phases instead of inventing their absence.

record_progress already checks writer.state == active INSIDE its transaction

exact generation/preparation/fence predicate that permits its own legitimate
revocation and replay while rejecting supersession and foreign revocation.

## P1: attempt custody holds do not account for writers of the selected line

The selected write target is persistent line_path. workspaces.line_assignment_workspace
at2850 explicitly identifies the line namespace as disjoint from custody roots;
its adoption guard protects attempt inputs, not line_path. Checking the old
attempt's workspace/result custody holds does not enumerate or exclude managers
restoring/freezing/writing this shared line. restore_abandoned_correction's
profile.restore_checkpoint is a concrete manager-owned writer of it.

Bind exclusion to the exact line object and generation and enumerate the actual
runtime/helper/manager writers capable of reaching that object. Specify how their
admission is blocked during revocation and how each writer's termination or
completed local effect is confirmed outside the DB lock. A committed
attempt.finalize-quiescent intent is an existing vocabulary to investigate, not
proof that every writer is terminated. Trace its producer and runtime observation
contract before claiming it is sufficient exclusion evidence. Unknowns remain held.

## P1: removal intent plus after-effect validation does not prevent deletion races

Change6 records intent, deletes outside the lock, then checks whether holds changed.
As written, no reciprocal admission rule makes custody claims/reuse refuse that
intent while the deletion is in flight. A hold can therefore arrive during deletion;
refusing settlement afterwards cannot undo lost bytes. The proposed test allowing
'a hold committed during the effect' must distinguish harmless state change from
forbidden conflicting writer admission, not accept destructive overlap.

Specify intent acquisition versus ALL conflicting admission paths atomically,
exact resource/generation binding, replay and crash recovery. A crashed remover
must remain excluded/held until its old external effect cannot continue; expiry
alone is no release. This is the same safety problem as writer replacement,
not merely moving removing() outside transact. Include both forced orders and
restart before/after effect, with byte/effect preservation assertions.
