## Proposed bounded correction

Prefer scheduler reconciliation of the existing completed integration account.
This avoids a new runtime-cleanup authorization with a different proof and
resource-destruction scope. It returns LOGICAL worker/principal capacity; it does
not claim the old container, credential or workspace has been destroyed or that
cleanup moved out of pending. Resource cleanup is outside this proposed slice.

Use a narrow predicate for a current integration stage whose validated projected
state and integration account are completed. Bind evidence to the allocation's
exact stage/episode/attempt and participant, and compare the completion runtime
identity to the observed runtime. Do not confuse pool generation with Authority
assignment generation. A new episode's completion must not release an older
allocation sharing its stage id. Invoke the existing idempotent release owner
with a distinct completion reason; do not directly update allocation rows.

Do not add generic completed to RELEASE_ENDINGS: ordinary worker completion
still owes intake/cleanup. Do not release on answered, integrated entry alone,
lease release alone, quiescence alone or incomplete/unbound completion. Earlier
failure guards remain; noncompleted uncertainty still quarantines capacity.
Proposed historical-completion policy follows the accepted observation contract:
once the exact completion is validated, a later runtime-axis uncertainty does
not make its ended assignment live again. Owner approval below must pin this
new scheduler use of that evidence before implementation.

No scheduler read of the integration or Authority store, no new observation
schema, no mutation by status, no fake cleanup receipt, no source bypass.

## Target revision is a distinct acceptance question

integration/admission.py resolved_account compares proposal.target with the
Authority's current canonical_target before enqueue. The driver's _settled_entry
replays a previously settled proposal without re-admitting it against a moved
target; it does not make another Job's stale proposal current. Thus capacity
return can unblock B admission while B legitimately still owes correction.

Proposed prerequisite acceptance: the real A terminal handoff releases only its
integration allocation on an ordinary tick; eligible B can then reserve that
same worker. Preserve any legitimate stale-target refusal and its evidence.
W130224/W119405 retain the full two-Job integration/correction acceptance. This
separates delivery ownership rather than weakening that final requirement.
Do not convert a stale proposal refusal into success or widen target policy here.
