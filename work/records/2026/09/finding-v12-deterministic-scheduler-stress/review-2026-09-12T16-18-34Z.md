`test_scheduler_trace.py:39-41,96-108,194-216,450-464`.

completion interleaving of the real serving sweep. The third exported schedule
is a capacity miss, not the requested reopen-and-continue schedule.

participants, all named `baton.*`. They are not configured Authority teams.

checkout, not independent repository bindings. With both implementation slots

`test_an_unrelated_review_is_eligible_while_implementation_is_full` never asks

completion receipt. This helper must not become the shortcut to making the
unfinished schedules pass.

exercise an unrelated eligible review, correction, completion and alternate
completion order, and export the reopen schedule. Reuse the accepted fixture

exact missing interface and retained partial evidence; do not substitute labels
