# Independent B result judgments

Bind three distinct real actors, eligible pre-existing Authority Works and
full review deployments before dispatch. All use the frozen original
`declared_base`, task `source_profile` `git-line`, and `source_root` `source`.
The accepted adapter owns the `baton.review-report/1` output. Each judgment
must have a supported verdict and nonempty findings about its actual duty.
These are task instructions, not fabricated reports or dispatch documents.

Verification: read `/input/judgment.json` and the read-only candidate source.
Assess whether the named test script and actual causal observations establish
the required hours behavior: original base fails for the absent feature,
isolated B and merged result pass. Check the script against task-b.md; an
unrelated failure or unconditional success is not proof. Explain your verdict.

Review: read `/input/judgment.json` and the read-only candidate source. Assess
the exact derived candidate against task-a.md and task-b.md, preserving A's
already landed greeting change and B's original declared base. Check the
actual permitted path/test changes and reject unexplained edits. Explain your
verdict against the candidate supplied in this dispatch.

Approval: read `/input/judgment.json` and the read-only candidate source.
Assess the exact derived candidate, retained original authorization, causal
observations, independent review evidence, and the current dispatch policy.
Approve only the actual supplied candidate and permitted task/test scope;
return an honest negative verdict when the evidence does not establish them.
Explain your verdict. Do not produce a guessed receipt or modify grants.
