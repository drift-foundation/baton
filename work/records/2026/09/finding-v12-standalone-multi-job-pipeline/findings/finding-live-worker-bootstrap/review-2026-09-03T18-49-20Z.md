credential recovery. When the launch root is absent after a start request,
`_launch_document` refuses at lines 651-660; `start` records that as a
preparation failure at lines 575-583. The new record deliberately authorizes no
cleanup, the Job projection becomes `exceptional`, and no later tick calls the
launch capability again. Nothing therefore identifies or stops the runtime
that the preceding process created, and nothing settles its live credential
lifecycle record.

The new regression at `tests/tools/test_single_worker.py:833-860` asserts that
the engine receives no request and that the stage ends, but does not assert
that the already-created runtime or credential lifecycle ended. Driving that
case through six later ticks leaves all of the following simultaneously true:

```text

credential lifecycle: live, runtime-single-1
launch root: absent
```

That is an unmanaged live worker carrying the credential mount, not a bounded
failure ending. Give this contradiction a safe owner operation which can prove
and stop the exact runtime without rereading/registering the bearer or repairing
the launch bytes, and prove the runtime plus credential lifecycle are settled.
If the pinned “reaches no engine” rule must change to do that, supersede it
explicitly before implementation rather than leaving two live decisions.
