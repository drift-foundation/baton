## [P1] Preparation replay discards credentials belonging to a running runtime

The new `_materialized` helper calls CredentialHome.discard_orphan whenever a
credential record or root exists. Its comment assumes the attempt is not-started,
but neither it nor prepare checks that assumption. record_attempt and
activate_assignment replay their existing acts and do not establish this runtime
precondition.

In the real composed fixture the manager says running and CredentialHome holds
a live record naming runtime-integrator-attempt. Repeating `port.prepare`:

- succeeds;
- calls the real discard_orphan once;
- asks the synthetic provider again;
- leaves the credential lifecycle record absent while the runtime remains running.

The wrapped call retained in the probe runs the actual owner's implementation.
No live credential or engine was used. `discard_orphan` explicitly requires its
caller to prove the root stale; a process's lack of an in-memory delivery is not
that proof. Even before start, identical preparation currently remints rather
than replaying the exact prepared capability.

Prove preparation eligibility through the public manager owner before any
credential discard/materialization. Reuse exact prepared delivery for an
identical in-process replay; preserve or refuse a started/uncertain/reconstructed
attempt without discarding its live credential custody. Add regressions that
measure provider calls and credential root/record preservation as well as the
attempt identity. Do not weaken the credential owner's stale-root requirement
or assume the caller will never repeat preparation after restart.

## [P1] The new refresh preflight reintroduces the witness-failure marker leak

The new initial `prior_runtime_witness` call is outside the try/except that was
introduced to cover the whole observation. When that call raises, the marker
survives. The retained probe injects a ContractRefusal at that public witness
read for a started attempt and measures may_continue=true afterwards.

Move the no-start check inside the complete invalidation boundary. Preserve the
intended no-start refusal without a reconcile_runtime write, and invalidate on
failure of either witness read, adoption, adapter construction or reconciliation.
This is the same whole-observation requirement accepted in the preceding review;
no new permission or recovery behavior is requested.
