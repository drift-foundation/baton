## P0 — erasing the block within the accepted schema advances the queue

`target_of` validates the blocked relationship only when
`targets.blocked_account` is present. `lease_of` validates an abandonment's
ending and attempt but does not prove that its target is still blocked or that
the target's blocked account names this lease. Consequently the opposite
halves of the same relationship remain independently acceptable:

- an `open` target can coexist with a `held` entry and an `abandoned` lease;
- the abandoned lease can be returned without the block it claims recovered;
  and
- `grant_lease` sees an open target and no live lease, then leases the next
  queued entry while the earlier entry remains held.

The re-review created a valid blocked/held account, performed its accounted
abandonment, then changed only the target row to the schema-valid open shape
(`state='open'`, both block columns null). The public reads accepted the split
state and the next grant succeeded:

```text
target-after-erased-block ACCEPTED open
abandonment-without-block ACCEPTED {'outcome': 'abandoned', ...}
next-grant-past-erased-block ACCEPTED entry-2 ['held', 'leased']
```

This is the prior serialization defect re-entering through restart evidence:
one unresolved held entry is bypassed by a later leased entry. Validating a
block only when the target says it has one lets the corrupted absence choose
the permissive branch.

Correction boundary: adopt the target/entry/lease state graph as one
relationship from every public read and before selection. In the item-3 state
model, an open target cannot coexist with a held entry or an abandonment born
from a block; an abandoned lease must cross-check the still-blocked target and
its exact blocked account. If a later repair operation reopens a target, that
operation must first durably reconcile the held entry and recovery account so
the invariant remains true. Avoid recursive partial validation by centralizing
raw-row adoption and performing one explicit relationship pass.

Add regressions that erase the block after live blocking and after
abandonment, call `target_of`, `lease_of`, and `grant_lease` independently,
and prove every door refuses without changing the held entry, lease, target
fence, or next queued entry. Also cover a fabricated open target with a held
entry and an abandoned lease so correctness does not depend on operation
history being available in process memory.
