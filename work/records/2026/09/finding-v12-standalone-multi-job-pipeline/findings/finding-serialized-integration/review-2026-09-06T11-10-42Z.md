## [P0] Every exact retry bypasses the semantic journal proof

**Observed.** `IntegrationStore.transact` calls `replay` before the action and
again under the write lock. `queue._recorded` also delegates directly to that
same method. `replay` compares only the caller's raw signature and optional
kind, then returns `json.loads(row["result"])`; it does not adopt the operation
row through `OPERATION_COLUMNS`, `_act`, `_result_of`, the materialized state,
or the target relationship. The new whole-operation proof therefore protects
ordinary reads and first executions but not the retry path that is specifically
supposed to make the transition effectively once.

Two reproductions show the consequences:

1. After a normal enqueue, changing only its recorded result's
   `candidate_digest` makes an exact call to `enqueue` return the rewritten
   candidate while the materialized entry still carries the original digest.
2. With an entry still `leased`, inserting a schema-valid committed
   `entry.integrated` operation carrying the exact settlement signature and a
   fabricated integrated result makes `settle_integrated` return success while
   the materialized entry remains `leased`.

The second case crosses the safety boundary this method owns: a caller receives
the scheduler's successful post-import settlement result without the durable
settlement that is required before Authority completion. A later ordinary
read refuses the contradiction, but that is after the transition already
reported success.

Correction boundary: make replay one semantically owned path. Before returning
a committed or refused outcome, adopt the complete operation row, derive and
check its canonical identity, own its exact operands/result or refusal variant,
and cross-bind the result to the materialized target/entry/lease effect under
the transaction's lock. Every public mutator, including the preflight retry
used by fenced verbs, must reach that path. Preserve exact replay before a
now-ended live-grant check, but do not make “replay first” mean “trust the raw
journal first.” Add corrupt-result, fabricated-terminal-act, malformed/NULL
refusal/result, and row/result mismatch retry cases for every operation family;
each must refuse without mutation, while sound retries still return the first
owned outcome.

## [P0] Generated rank and selection are not proved by the signed act

**Observed.** The enqueue request cannot contain its transactionally generated
rank. `_result_of("entry.enqueue")` therefore checks only that the result rank
is a positive generation, and `_agrees_with_the_journal` checks that the same
rank appears in the materialized row. No surviving evidence proves the rank
that transaction actually allocated or that a grant result selected the
smallest queued rank.

After two normal enqueues at ranks 1 and 2, changing entry 1's rank to 3 in
both the entry row and its enqueue result is accepted by `entries_of`. The
queue is returned as entry 2 at rank 2 followed by entry 1 at rank 3, and
`grant_lease` grants entry 2. The signed enqueue still names entry 1's original
request, but the coordinated rewrite has reversed durable FIFO order and the
new proof has no independent allocation witness to contradict it.

This is the same missing relationship for other generated members: type-
checking a rank/fence/instant and making result equal row does not prove that it
was the value allocated by the signed operation. At minimum, the relationship
must anchor enqueue order/rank and grant selection/fence to durable ordering
evidence that a row-plus-result rewrite cannot change, then prove that evidence
at every read, enqueue and selection door. Add coordinated row/result rank
rewrite, missing/duplicate rank, grant-result retargeting past a lower queued
rank, and generated fence/order rewrite cases. Each refusal must leave the
queue, lease history, fence and journal unchanged. First execution, exact
replay and same-target FIFO must remain positive controls.
