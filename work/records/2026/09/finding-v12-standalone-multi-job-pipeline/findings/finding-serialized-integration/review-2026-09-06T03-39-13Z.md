signature untouched, then change `checkpoint_id` and `candidate_digest` in


This is the prior immutable-account P0 with two redundant surfaces changed
instead of one. The original signed request is still present and proves the
contradiction, so selection must not trust the mutually agreeing row and result

**Cause.** `_history` proves only the signature's top-level kind and member
set. `_agrees_with_the_journal` compares an enqueue result to the entry row,
but never compares `operands.eligibility`, target, or entry id to that result.

terminal proofs use the presence of globally collected operation identities
without binding their signed operands and results to the entry/lease state.
Operation identities are also not re-derived from the adopted operands, so a
noncanonical identity can masquerade as another committed act or create a
second act for one logical subject.

## Required correction boundary

Adopt each committed journal kind through one semantic owner that:

- validates every operand with the same identity, generation, target,
  eligibility, settlement, ending and recovery rules used at admission;
- derives and checks the canonical operation identity from those owned
  operands;
- adopts the result as the exact per-kind result variant, including a typed
  empty-grant result where legitimate and typed refusal of SQL NULL or any
  malformed non-result;
- cross-binds the signed request to its result and materialized row, including
  activation document/digest, enqueue target/id/complete eligibility, grant
  target/id/holder/attempt, and every terminal act's exact target/entry/lease/
  fence/account;
- rejects duplicate committed acts for one logical activation, entry, lease,
  settlement or ending instead of silently overwriting one in a dictionary;
  and
- applies the same exact binding to the pending act rather than treating its
  operation identity alone as sufficient provenance for a new row.

Generated rank, fence and instants necessarily come from the result rather
than the signed request, but the result must first be proved to be the result
of that exact signed act. Existing row/result comparison then remains the
third side of the same relationship.

Add regressions for each operation kind's wrong operand type, operand/result
mismatch, noncanonical operation id, duplicate logical act, SQL-null/wrong-
variant result, and exact pending-act mismatch. Retain the row-plus-result
rewrite above as the selection and duplicate-admission P0. Each refusal must
leave rows and journal unchanged, while first execution, exact replay, and a
reopened sound store still pass.
