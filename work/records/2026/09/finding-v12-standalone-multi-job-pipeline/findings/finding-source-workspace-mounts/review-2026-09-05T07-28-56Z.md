and four persisted identity columns. Its only edits to

all 63 modules and ran 520 shards: 3,634 tests, 6 failures, 0 errors, 3 skips.

  member, `filesystem_of`, `nominate_source`, and `source_mountpoint`.
- `EveryReceivingEntryHasOneOwner.test_every_receiving_entry_has_an_owning_validator`
  reports the new `attempts.py:pin_boundary_identity` and
  `attempts.py:boundary_identity_of` operands, OCI `source_delivery`, the
  `NominatedSource`, `SourceBoundary`, and `WorkspaceCapacity` constructors,
  the source-boundary composition/adoption/mount operations, and the new
  workspace capability operations as receiving entries with no owner.

These entries did not exist at base—the entire `source_boundary.py` file and
the four identity columns are new in this candidate. A pre-existing nonzero

gate, even though the names of the failing test methods are unchanged.

failure must be shown to contain no candidate-introduced path, operand, member,
or column—not merely have the same test name or count as the baseline. The
correction changes frozen bytes, so this digest must be superseded by a new
manifest and fresh review.
