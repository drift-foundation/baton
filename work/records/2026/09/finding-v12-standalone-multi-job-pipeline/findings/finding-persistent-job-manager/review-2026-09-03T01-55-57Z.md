## Correction audit

The pass-3 correction closes the reported distinct-offer/one-attempt defect.
`ManagerOperations.observe` retains the claimed offer identity as
`claimed_by`; `_one_claim` refuses an impossible legacy store containing more
than one claimed offer rather than choosing by row order. `observation_of`
proves this stage's offer and passes the acquired observation through `_bound`:

- another offer holding the derived attempt refuses
  `refused/operation-collision`, naming both offers and the attempt;
- this stage's own offer holding it permits the canonical runtime, activity,
  and output facts; and
- no holder causes every attempt-keyed fact to be projected as absent, so a
  claim or runtime arriving after the holder read cannot be misattributed in
  that pass.

That ordering is sufficient without a cross-store lock. Once this stage's own
offer is observed as the holder, `offers_one_claim_per_attempt` prevents a
foreign offer from becoming claimed on the same attempt. If no holder was
observed, `_bound` discards the later attempt reads; the next pass either sees
the rightful holder or refuses a foreign one.

`projection.stage_states` now calls only `observation_of`; an independent
source search found no remaining direct `operations.observe` call or stale
boolean `claimed` member in the Job-manager implementation. Status,
`owed_acts`, and `sweep` therefore share the same fail-closed binding.

The six new cases cover the real two-offer/one-attempt status, owed-act,
receipt, and own-offer paths plus the unclaimed-attempt rule. The recorded
mutations separately prove the holder comparison and unclaimed gating and
retain the pass-1/pass-2 regressions.
