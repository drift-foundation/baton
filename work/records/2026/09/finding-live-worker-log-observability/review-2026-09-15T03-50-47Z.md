## P1 — terminal precheck does not fence an in-flight store write

Section3/4 says the consumer terminal predicate makes late writes harmless and
ignores same-operation documents after terminal. It only describes a check
before ingestion. Current attempts.observe_activity (:427-494) unconditionally
accepts a greater total for an existing attempt; its UPDATE has no terminal or
publisher-generation predicate. Repeat/regression rules do not reject a new
greater total.

**Reasoned interleaving, not an executed probe:** ingestion observes nonterminal
work and total100, then stalls opening/writing the store; terminal appears and
the composition closes; the abandoned ingestion resumes and records100 over
the previous50 with a new manager receipt instant. Exact operation binding and
the earlier terminal check still pass. Checking terminal again just before the
call only shortens this race. Restart is not a revocation of that old writer.

Finish the semantics rather than claim this window closed. Either explicitly
permit a delayed final count admitted while live (receipt time then means delayed
manager observation, not continued provider activity), or identify a real
linearization/revocation boundary that rejects the write when its authority has
expired. The latter may require a bounded owner/composition amendment;
attempts.py cannot be promised unchanged while assuming an atomic eligibility
condition it does not expose. Do not hold a shutdown/sweep lock across blocked
I/O to simulate that guarantee. This is a concrete unresolved design boundary,
not a demand for an exhaustive race campaign. The already-selected focused
delayed-write case must exercise a write paused after the eligibility read.
