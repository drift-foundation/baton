## Final correction accepted

Both actual factories use the same process-wide helper ownership primitive,
keyed by the held control-store identity with no attempt id. They acquire
atomically and without waiting before starting a helper. An occupied slot
suppresses helper creation and all activity ingestion in the new composition.
The slot remains occupied until positive termination; partial construction may
release it only if the helper never started. Per-attempt identity checks remain
request checks and no longer decide whether a helper may exist.

This excludes the C1/H1/A versus C2/H2/B sequence from review175005. Planned
acceptance now explicitly uses different attempts and proves no second helper
starts while the first is unresolved. The owning ingestion thread closes its
own store handle during finalization. A timed-out serving closer leaves that
handle and ownership slot alone and reports the unresolved helper. No separate
cross-thread close or process-wide/cross-process guarantee is inferred beyond
the stated same-process store-level exclusion.
