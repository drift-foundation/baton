can clone a detached bind object; `move_mount` can attach that object; and
`mount_setattr` sets its private/nosuid/nodev properties. Their availability is
not proof that the local kernel/security configuration permits the experiment.
See [open_tree(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/open_tree.2.html),
[move_mount(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/move_mount.2.html), and
[mount_setattr(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/mount_setattr.2.html).

failure requiring shutdown, with no stronger-flags retry. Namespace-entry authority
is host-side and separately approved. See
[umount(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/umount.2.html) and
[setns(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/setns.2.html).
