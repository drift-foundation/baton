## Pinned implementation-plan clarification

Within the accepted per-Job timeout scope, use an injected, read-only execution
context callable composed by the factory over its already-open Job owner. This
refines PLAN item3's placement of resolution; it does not change the owner's
accepted timeout behavior. Preserve full launch.adopt canonical-byte comparison.

1. Keep the Job-store handle in the deployment/factory. Hand _SingleWorker only
   the narrowly named context callable, threaded through worker_operations and
   its bootstrap/pooled/per-Job integration construction sites. The callable uses
   submission.job_execution_context or the same public Job-owner readers. It
   exposes no store write, migration, Authority session, engine or credentials.
2. Give private launch/adoption helpers the stage/attempt context they need.
   _prepared, command, ending and observed_exchange all route through the same
   reconstruction. No mutable current-Job field or admission-only memory cache:
   a fresh process must obtain the same expected Job context.
3. Observation factories compose the same narrow read from their already-open
   read-only Job owner. _Observation retains the callable rather than the raw
   store. Keep observe_exchange(stage) unchanged. Do not construct the serving
   factory, open a writable store, perform migration/refresh, mint a session or
   add the historical extra configuration/Authority refusal. Update the local
   no-Job-read comment to describe this bounded new dependency and reference
   this decision; preserve the old Authority-check decision distinctly.
4. Bind stage.job_id/attempt_id to the real owned attempt. Runtime input/policy
   identities must be the actual retained claim/attempt and configured/prepared
   manifest identities, not the Job's original digests copied over them. The
   existing _SingleWorker._claim compares claimed_offers_for against configured
   input/policy; use equivalent public read-only evidence in observation. Honor
   pool allocation and per-Job integration configuration when choosing those
   operands. Never extract expected Job settings from the launch being checked.
5. Re-resolve the Job's pinned generation/request through its owner and pass the
   resulting complete context into launch.materialize/adopt. Missing/mismatched
   context in a Job-bound path fails closed; it is never an excuse to adopt a
   configured Job as a legacy launch. Preserve /1 and /2 for explicit legacy
   callers and the existing missing-post-start-delivery refusal. Unallocated or
   not-yet-started stages retain their ordinary absence semantics. Damaged
   material remains isolated to that stage's existing unreadable observation.
6. Derived JudgmentExecution receives its owning result Job explicitly from
   stage_execution and binds settings into retained intent/dispatch identity.
   It is not a Job stage: do not invent a stage-to-Job lookup for it. Supply its
   context callable or immutable explicit context using its own attempt/input
   identities. Keep direct integration's whole-launch bundle binding intact.

This is the operand/capability form of the requested seam correction. It neither
hands the worker a general Job-store handle nor weakens adoption to trusting the
Job half found on disk. It requires no frozen control/session/exchange protocol
change and no ManagerOperations/projection callback signature change. The source
paths are already in the accepted finite scope: single_worker.py,
stage_execution.py, integration_worker.py, integration_bundle.py and the existing
Job-reader/launch modules. A callable name is an implementation detail; its
authority and reconstruction requirements above are the pinned boundaries.
