## P1 — cleanup replay does not own nested directory custody

`review_driver._let_go` accepts the replayed receipt's directory custody if it
has result/workspace keys with dictionary values. It does not validate those
root records against their historical custody owner. The independent probe
performs an actual ordinary ending, reopens its store, and replaces only the
cleanup journal receipt's two root records with empty dictionaries. Resume
returns success with no external act or new control write. The actual owned
custody remains available but is never compared to this substituted receipt.

Required correction: prove the nested directory records and exact operation,
assignment, root and custody relationships through the public owner. Existing
`historical_directory_custody` is relevant; the private `_cleaned_review`
precedent compares owned historical evidence, not just container types. A
replayed operation's input signature is not validation of its stored result.
Missing, malformed and foreign custody must refuse before returning success.

## P1 — retained proposal data does not prove publication committed

`_published` reads a retained proposalManifest and compares attempt/result/
assignment selectors. That proves a proposal was retained, not that Authority
publication completed. `integration.driver.retain_proposal` commits this very
manifest BEFORE `publish_candidate` calls the publisher and verifies its
answer/readback. Its publish_receipt_digest is computed from the expected
answer, not a receipt acquired by performing publication.

The new fixture's `_RetainedPublication.publish` composes its own manifest and
calls `retain_manifest`; it never calls `publish_candidate` or an Authority
publication. Thus its positive historical proof cannot distinguish retained-
but-unpublished from published. This is a confirmed source/fixture limitation,
not a claim of a reproduced ordinary production crash reaching false success.

Required correction: a durable owner record that is committed only after the
real publication and exact readback succeed, with a local read-only historical
reader. Retained-only must answer absence/refusal. Keep ordinary return
semantics and bind result/assignment/proposal/manifest and the actual accepted
publication evidence. The component proof must exercise the real publication
owner over a properly declared proposal output, with named deterministic
transport stand-ins, rather than manufacture that owner's evidence.

## P2 — missing cleanup still reaches the Authority

The author explicitly measures one assignment_of Authority read when no
committed destroy exists. `_let_go` calls `authorize_cleanup`; if replay misses,
its next branch queries the Authority. A mutable cleanup guard cannot guarantee

including refusal cases. Reporting the residual correctly does not discharge
it. A separately owned public local cleanup reader is required; the proposed
scope is in `PROVIDER-SPLIT-PROPOSAL-2026-09-08.md`.

receipt-artifact decisions, including decisions for discarded artifacts.
Retain positive retain and discard controls, plus incomplete-set refusals.
