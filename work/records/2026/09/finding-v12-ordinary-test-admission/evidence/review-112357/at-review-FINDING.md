## 2026-09-07 — implementation adoption and correction — baton.claude

The accepted contract is implemented as written; the review of claim112186
found three real defects and they are corrected. Four decisions are pinned
here because the review asked for the adopted choices to be on the record.

**Unrun is explicitly null.** The observation carries `status: null` when the
command produced no status at all -- skipped, timed out, or unable to start.
A missing member would say the same thing by omission, which a closed reader
cannot tell from a producer that forgot to write one, and the consumer must
distinguish "no evidence" from "evidence of failure".

**The emitted verification identities carry an explicit prefix.**
`ordinary-tests-verification`, so the marker is visible in the opaque id an
operator reads rather than only hashed into the basis. Review and approval
identities are unchanged: what this Work altered is which evidence a
VERIFICATION receipt rests on, and relabelling the other two would claim
something about acts it did not touch.

**The proposal's whole producer identity and its target are compared before any
receipt.** `resolved_account` makes several of these comparisons, but
`admit_accepted` has already written three immutable receipts by then, and a
later admission refusal cannot retract one. The comparisons therefore live in
this Work's own reader, ahead of the side effect.

**The conditional tenth path needed no edit.** `required_tests` is a keyword of
`admit_accepted`, which the dependency inventory already covers through its
existing `required` operand; declaring anything would have produced a stale
entry that the staleness gate beside it refuses.
