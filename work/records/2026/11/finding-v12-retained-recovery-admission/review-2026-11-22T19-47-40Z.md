## F1 — BLOCKING: the admitted successor's review is immediately dispatchable, before any verification

`_composed` submits the successor as ONE `review` stage with
`depends_on: []` (retained_recovery.py ~1642). `projection.gates_of` makes a
stage with no gates `queued` (projection.py ~740), so the owed acts include
an offer.

Probe P3 drove the real `manager.sweep` with `fixtures.FakeOperations` right
after `recovery-decide --approve` (stages verification, review). It performed
`admit`/`offer.issue` for `recovery-D1/review`, and the state became
`offered`. That happened in the first tick, with no verification executed or
journalled.

The admission answer says `readiness.verification = "owed"` and that review
consumes the candidate "once that verification passes". Nothing enforces
that, so the readiness answer is untrue as soon as a serving manager ticks.
The review stage also carries no binding to the retained candidate. It has
no dependency and no input naming the candidate, so a configured reviewer
would be offered a review with nothing linking it to the exact bytes.

This contradicts:
- the STAGE-1-CONTRACT ordering, in which successful verification hands the
  candidate to review;
- REC-8 verification/review-only recovery;
- the task's "Expose truthful readiness/refusal at the admission/execution
  boundary";
- M385684: an "unsupported execution binding cannot appear runnable".

On a real deployment, approval would dispatch a reviewer immediately.

**Required.** For the verification-and-review set, the admitted successor
must not be offerable until W385625's verification has journalled a passed
account for that exact successor and the candidate handoff exists. For the
review-only set, the review must be bound to the exact retained candidate and
the lineage. Either use a durable gate that `projection`/`sweep` honors, or
admit no runnable stage until 1-C supplies its execution. Readiness must
match what the scheduler will actually do.

**Verify** with the real `sweep`/tick:
- no offer is made for the successor while verification is owed or unknown;
- a failed or unknown account never opens review;
- a cancelled or expired successor is never offered;
- review-only offers only with valid lineage evidence;
- an existing Job's gating is unchanged.
