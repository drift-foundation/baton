## Correction

- **C1 (recommended, not blocking).** `completed_turn` splits the stream
  with `str.splitlines()`, which also breaks at U+2028, U+2029, U+0085 and
  other separators that are legal unescaped inside a JSON string. A real
  successful turn whose message or command output contains one is reported
  unclassified (probe: `text_with_U2028`, `text_with_U0085` → false).
  Split on the newline byte only. It errs toward failure, never toward
  success.
