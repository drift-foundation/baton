## First deliverable: retained clarification/finding → reviewer answer → continuation

Implement one vertical native communication path: an implementer can stop at a question or an in-scope finding, retain its exact checkpoint and continuation, hand the request directly to its configured reviewer, receive an attributable answer, and resume the same task without human relay. The answer can resolve an interpretation within accepted scope or classify a finding as continue-within-scope. A reviewer that cannot resolve it records one explicit escalation with evidence/options/recommendation and leaves the execution held for the configured authorized decision recipient. A text message alone never transfers ownership or approves code.

This first slice includes durable Work/Job-scoped request/answer messages, readback for agents/humans, typed handoff and continuation state, exact retention and restart recovery, explicit finite continuation policy, and honest status. Ordinary final implementation→technical review and review→correction semantics are reused, not replaced. No separate generic chat system, activity-log parser, second authority or coordinator-agent relay.

This is deliberately NOT the full first-discovery acceptance milestone. The immediately following slice adds the nonblocking-file and atomic blocking-prerequisite dispositions and accepted-base-gated resume. Until that slice is accepted, a finding needing Work filing or dependency treatment remains visibly awaiting supported classification; it is not coerced to continue or success. ## Clarification, decisions and routing

Clarification preserves admitted task/spec digest, base and scope; append the answer to continuation input as a separately digested message. Reopening an invocation with that answer does not rewrite the task. A genuine scope/spec change follows authorized revision/new-admission behavior and is never smuggled into clarification. `reject_plan` is inappropriate for an ordinary question: its gate requires a DIFFERENT plan digest.

Preserve actual worker identities `baton.claude-native` and `baton.reviewer-native`; keep human authority `baton.slaw`. Route handlers, receipt actors and permission grantees are not synonyms. In particular `baton.reviewer-receipt-native` is not automatically the agent answering a question. Bind any new respond/classify permission explicitly to the intended native reviewer in the relevant Work scope, through bootstrap's configuration/grant owner. Existing `review` or route membership grants no new capability by implication. A request is authenticated against the requesting assignment and its configured allowed destinations.

Routine question: implementation→configured reviewer→same producer's committed route, automatically. A reviewer unable to resolve it records an escalation addressed to the configured higher decision authority, currently the owner baton.slaw; do not route `decision` back to that same reviewer under another label. One durable pending escalation per request, no recursive self-route and no acknowledgment polling requirement. Re-entry can reread/replay it, not create a fresh escalation each tick. Do not invent a new decision worker/route by inserting a string: current bootstrap roles are implementation/review/integration. Additional machine decision roles would require explicit supported configuration/admission, not a hidden intermediary.

## Finite continuation policy

Do not implement continuation by calling `open_correction` or forging a changes-requested verdict. Current correction accounting counts CORRECT_KIND operations and documentation/1 permits zero. A continuation is a separately admitted action and must have its own finite allowance; it consumes no code-correction allowance, does not reset stage/Job clocks, and must obey cancellation, timing uncertainty, cleanup and runtime-token fences.

For the minimal new handoff-capable profile, select as the reviewer planning default **one resolved continuation** (one additional implementation invocation and its necessary response invocation), retain documentation/1's zero code-correction rounds and 4800/9960/3600/900-second stage/Job/provider/verification ceilings, with no deadline reset. Add a NEW immutable profile/contract revision; do not widen documentation/1 in place. This is a minimal bounded implementation-policy choice for the one-question demonstration, not authority for an extra live run. Exhaustion must hold/report honestly; replay does not spend twice. Genuine changes-requested at final technical review still cannot open a correction under a zero-correction profile. Later selection of positive correction allowances is explicit policy selection; the system must honor it, not manufacture a human permission gate or bypass a zero allowance.

The native Job that IMPLEMENTS this feature can use today's documentation/1 and existing final-proposal contract: it produces ordinary source/test changes and is independently reviewed under existing machinery. It does not itself exercise the new runtime protocol live. Deterministic fixtures exercise the new admitted profile; deployment qualification follows source acceptance. If that development Job receives changes-requested, preserve the verdict/candidate and use the already supported bounded correction/fresh-attempt selection process; do not claim zero permits a successor.
