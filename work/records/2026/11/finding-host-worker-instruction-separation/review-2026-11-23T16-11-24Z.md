## Required changes

### R1 — The rule for recorded owner exceptions is cut to half a sentence (root `AGENTS.md`, line 16)

- **Baseline (lines 658–669):** an explicit owner exception recorded in
  the owning FINDING overrides the general execution boundary, but only
  within its stated scope and lifetime; it must be revalidated before
  acting; it is not inferred from a blocker and not extended to successor
  or sibling Work; moving a ruling out of the instructions neither revokes
  it nor revives an expired one.
- **Candidate:** "Keep history and temporary exceptions in the owning
  dossier; a blocker grants no exception."
- **What is lost:** the permission (a recorded exception does govern
  within its bounds) and three restrictions (scope and lifetime;
  revalidate before acting; no extension to sibling or successor Work).
  The matrix row for these lines says "revalidate scope/lifetime;
  relocation does not revoke or revive", but that text is not in the
  candidate. This rule matters more after the consolidation than before
  it, because every temporary ruling now lives only in a dossier.
- **Required:** restore those four points in the root, in current-rule
  wording.

### R2 — "builds" in the worker document restricts what DESIGN permits (`docs/V12-WORKER-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, line 66)

- **Candidate:** "Live models, engines, builds, deployment changes and
  broad runs require selection by the task".
- **Conflict:** DESIGN RUN-4 lets a worker use compilers and tests in its
  private area without per-command approval, and the baseline's list of
  separately selected execution is live models, engine operations outside
  scope and broad stress. A worker reading "builds" literally would stop
  before compiling the project it was asked to change. That is a new
  restriction, not a preserved one.
- **Required:** say what is meant — worker or container image builds — or
  remove the word.
