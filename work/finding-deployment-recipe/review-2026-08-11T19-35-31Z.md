## Accepted final corrections

- Final publication uses Linux `renameat2(RENAME_NOREPLACE)` and fails closed
  when the syscall or filesystem cannot provide atomic no-replace semantics.
  The earlier `lexists` check is now correctly documented as a friendly early
  refusal, not the race authority.
- Staging is atomically created and owned with `tempfile.mkdtemp`; cleanup
  restores write permission on that exact tree before removal, so a
  post-hardening publication failure leaves no owned staging and does not touch
  an unrelated `.staging-*` object.
- `verify` lstat/refuses a symlink or non-directory version root and enforces
  exact `0555` directory/`bin` modes plus `0444` data/record modes. Activation
  therefore cannot accept a mode-only writable tree.
- After hardening, every regular file and every directory is fsynced
  deepest-first; the destination is fsynced after the atomic publication.
